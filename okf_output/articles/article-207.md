# Article 207 -- Estimates of Revenues and Expenditures

> **Part 16:** Province Financial Procedures  
> **Page:** 106

### Clause (1)

(1) The Provincial Minister for Finance
shall, in respect of every fiscal year, lay before the Provincial Assembly an annual
estimate setting out, inter alia, the following matters:-

#### Sub-clause (1)(a)

(a)
An estimate of revenues;

#### Sub-clause (1)(b)

(b)
The moneys required to meet the charges on the Provincial
Consolidated Fund; and

#### Sub-clause (1)(c)

(c)
The moneys required to meet the expenditure to be provided for by a
Provincial Appropriation Act.

### Clause (2)

(2) The annual estimate to be laid pursuant to clause (1) shall also be
accompanied by a statement of the expenses allocated to every Ministry in the
previous fiscal year and particulars of whether or not the objectives of the expenses
have been achieved.
