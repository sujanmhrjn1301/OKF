# Article 82 -- Transaction of Business of Government of Nepal

> **Part 7:** Federal Executive  
> **Page:** 45

### Clause (1)

(1) The business of the Government
of Nepal shall be allocated and transacted in accordance with the rules approved by
the Government of Nepal.

### Clause (2)

(2) No question may be raised in any court as to whether or not the rules
referred to in clause (1) have been observed.
