# Article 10 -- Not to Deprive of Citizenship

> **Part 2:** Citizenship  
> **Page:** 5

### Clause (1)

(1) No citizen of Nepal shall be deprived of the right to
obtain citizenship.

### Clause (2)

(2) Provision of single federal citizenship with provincial identity has been
made in Nepal.
