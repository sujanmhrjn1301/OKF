# Article 206 -- Expenditures Chargeable on Province Consolidated Fund

> **Part 16:** Province Financial Procedures  
> **Page:** 105

#### Sub-clause (a)

(a)
The amount required as remuneration and facilities payable to the
Speaker of Province and the Deputy Speaker of Province;

#### Sub-clause (b)

(b)
The amount required as remuneration and facilities payable to the
Chairperson and members of the Provincial Public Service
Commission;

#### Sub-clause (c)

(c)
All charges relating to debts to which the Provincial Government is
liable;

#### Sub-clause (d)

(d)
Any sum required to satisfy any judgment or decree made by a court
against the Provincial Government; and

#### Sub-clause (e)

(e)
Any other sum specified by the provincial law to be chargeable on the
Province Consolidated Fund.
