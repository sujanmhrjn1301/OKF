# Article 124 -- Federal Contingency Fund

> **Part 10:** Federal Financial Procedures  
> **Page:** 66

### Clause (1)

(1) A Federal Act may create a fund to be known as the
contingency fund into which such moneys as may be determined by the Federal Act
shall be deposited from time to time.

### Clause (2)

(2) The fund referred to in the clause (1) shall be under the control of the
Government of Nepal and may meet any unforeseen expenditure out of such fund.

### Clause (3)

(3) The amount of the expenditure referred to in clause (2) shall be
reimbursed as soon as possible as provided for in the Federal Act.
