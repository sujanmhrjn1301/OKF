# Article 235 -- Coordination between Federation, Province and Local Level

> **Part 20:** Interrelations between Federation, Province and Local Level  
> **Page:** 121

### Clause (1)

(1) The Federal
Parliament shall make necessary laws in order to maintain coordination between the
Federation, Province and Local Level.

### Clause (2)

(2) The Provincial Assembly may maintain coordination between the Province
and Rural Municipalities or Municipalities and settle political disputes, if any, that
have arisen, in coordination with the concerned Rural Municipality, Municipality and
the District Coordination Committee.

### Clause (3)

(3) The processes and procedures for the settlement of disputes referred to in
clause (2) shall be as provided for in the provincial law.
