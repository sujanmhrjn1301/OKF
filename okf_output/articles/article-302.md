# Article 302 -- Formation and Operation of Government Services at Province and Local Levels

> **Part 33:** Transitional Provisions  
> **Page:** 180

### Clause (1)

(1)
The Government of Nepal shall make necessary provisions for the delivery of services
at the Province and Local Levels.

### Clause (2)

(2) The Government of Nepal may, in making provisions pursuant to clause

### Clause (1)

(1), arrange for the delivery of services by making adjustment of the employees
serving in the government services at the time of commencement of this Constitution
with the Federal, Provincial and Local Levels in accordance with law.
