# Article 155 -- Provisions relating to Conditions of Service and Facilities

> **Part 11:** Judiciary  
> **Page:** 81

Provisions relating to the
remuneration, facilities and conditions of service of the employees of the Federal
Judicial Service shall be as provided for in a Federal Act.
