# Article 264 -- Muslim Commission

> **Part 27:** Other Commissions  
> **Page:** 154

### Clause (1)

(1) There shall be a Muslim Commission of Nepal, consisting of
a Chairperson and four other members in the maximum.

### Clause (2)

(2) The President shall, on the recommendation of the Constitutional Council,
appoint the Chairperson and members of the Muslim Commission.

### Clause (3)

(3) The term of office of the Chairperson and members of the Muslim
Commission shall be six years from the date of appointment.

### Clause (4)

(4) Other matters relating to the qualification for the Chairperson and
members of the Muslim Commission, circumstances in which their offices fall vacant,

their remuneration and conditions of service and the functions, duties and powers of
this Commission shall be as provided for in the federal law.
