# Article 276 -- Pardons

> **Part 32:** Miscellaneous  
> **Page:** 164

The President may, in accordance with law, grant pardons, suspend,
commute or remit any sentence passed by any Court, judicial or quasi-judicial
institution or administrative authority or institution.
