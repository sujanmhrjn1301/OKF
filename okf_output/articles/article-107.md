# Article 107 -- Secretariat of Federal Parliament

> **Part 8:** Federal Legislature  
> **Page:** 58

There shall be a Secretariat for conducting and
managing the business of the Federal Parliament. The establishment of such
Secretariat and other matters related thereto shall be as provided for in the federal law.
