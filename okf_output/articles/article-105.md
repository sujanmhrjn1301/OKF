# Article 105 -- Restriction on Discussion

> **Part 8:** Federal Legislature  
> **Page:** 58

#### Proviso-1

> Provided that nothing in this Article shall be deemed to bar the expression of
opinions about the conduct of a Judge during deliberations held on a motion of
impeachment.
