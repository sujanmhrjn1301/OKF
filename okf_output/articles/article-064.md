# Article 64 -- Qualification for President

> **Part 6:** President and Vice-President  
> **Page:** 39

### Clause (1)

(1) A person who has the following qualifications shall be
eligible to become the President:-

#### Sub-clause (1)(a)

(a)   One who is eligible to become a member of the Federal Parliament;

#### Sub-clause (1)(b)

(b)   On who has completed the age of at least forty-five years; and

#### Sub-clause (1)(c)

(c)  One who is not disqualified by any law.

### Clause (2)

(2) Notwithstanding anything contained in clause (1), a person who has
already been elected twice as the President shall not be eligible to be a candidate in
the election to the President.
