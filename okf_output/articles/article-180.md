# Article 180 -- Vacation of Seat of a Member of Provincial Assembly

> **Part 14:** Provincial Legislature  
> **Page:** 94

#### Sub-clause (a)

(a)
In case he or she tenders resignation in writing to the Speaker of
Province;

#### Sub-clause (b)

(b)
In case he or she is no longer qualified or ceases to possess the
qualifications referred to in Article 178;

#### Sub-clause (c)

(c)
In case the term of the Provincial Assembly expires or it is dissolved;

#### Sub-clause (d)

(d)
In case he or she is absent in ten consecutive meetings without giving
information to the Provincial Assembly;

#### Sub-clause (e)

(e)
In case the political party of which he or she was a member when elected
provides a notification as provided for in the federal law that he or she
has defected from the party;

#### Sub-clause (f)

(f)
In case he or she dies.
