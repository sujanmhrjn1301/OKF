# Article 150 -- District Judges not to be engaged in any Other Office and Provisions relating to Transfer

> **Part 11:** Judiciary  
> **Page:** 78

### Clause (1)

(1) No District Judge shall be engaged in or deputed to any office other than
that of Judge.

#### Proviso-1

> Provided that the Government of Nepal may, in consultation with the Judicial
Council, depute a District Judge to work concerning judicial inquiry, or to legal or
judicial investigation or research and to any election related work for a specified
period.

### Clause (2)

(2) The Chief Justice may, on recommendation of the Judicial Council,
transfer a District Judge from one District Court to another District Court.
