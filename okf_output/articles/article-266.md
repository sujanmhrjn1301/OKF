# Article 266 -- National Security Council

> **Part 28:** Provisions Relating to National Security  
> **Page:** 156

### Clause (1)

(1) There shall be a National Security Council for making
recommendation to the Government of Nepal, Council of Ministers for the
formulation of a policy on overall national interest, security and defence of Nepal, and
for the mobilization or control of the Nepal Army, which shall consist of the following
as the Chairperson and members:-

#### Sub-clause (1)(a)

(a)
The Prime Minister
      - Chairperson

#### Sub-clause (1)(b)

(b)
Minister for Defence, Government of Nepal
       - Member

#### Sub-clause (1)(c)

(c)
Minister for Home, Government of Nepal
        - Member

#### Sub-clause (1)(d)

(d)
Minister for Foreign Affairs, Government of Nepal
-Member

#### Sub-clause (1)(e)

(e)
Minister for Finance, Government of Nepal
         - Member

#### Sub-clause (1)(f)

(f)
Chief Secretary to Government of Nepal
         - Member

#### Sub-clause (1)(g)

(g)
Chief of the Army Staff, Nepal Army
         - Member

### Clause (2)

(2) The secretary at the Ministry of Defence shall act as the member secretary
to the National Security Council.

### Clause (3)

(3) The National Security Council shall submit its annual report to the
President, and the President shall cause the report to be laid through the Prime
Minister before the Federal Parliament.

### Clause (4)

(4) Other matters relating to the National Security Council shall be as provided
for in the federal law.
