# Article 66 -- Functions, Duties and Powers of President

> **Part 6:** President and Vice-President  
> **Page:** 39

### Clause (1)

(1) The President shall exercise such
powers and perform such duties as conferred on him or her in accordance with this
Constitution or the federal law.

### Clause (2)

(2) In exercising the powers or duties under clause (1), the President shall
perform all other functions to be performed by him or her on recommendation and
with the consent of the Council of Ministers than those functions specifically provided
to be performed on recommendation of any institution or official under this

Constitution or federal law. Such recommendation and consent shall be submitted
through the Prime Minister.

### Clause (3)

(3) Any decision or order to be issued in the name of the President under
clause (2) and other instrument of authorization pertaining thereto shall be
authenticated as provided for in the federal law.
