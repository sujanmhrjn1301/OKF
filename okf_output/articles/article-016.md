# Article 16 -- Right to Live with Dignity

> **Part 3:** Fundamental Rights and Duties  
> **Page:** 8

### Clause (1)

(1) Every person shall have the right to live with dignity.

### Clause (2)

(2) No law shall be made providing for the death penalty to anyone.
