# Article 188 -- Provisions relating to Vote of Confidence and Motion of No confidence

> **Part 14:** Provincial Legislature  
> **Page:** 98

### Clause (1)

(1) The
Chief Minister may, whenever he or she considers necessary or appropriate to show
that he or she has confidence from the Provincial Assembly, table a resolution to that
effect in the Provincial Assembly for the vote of confidence.

### Clause (2)

(2) In case the political party which the Chief Minister represents is divided or
a political party in coalition Provincial Government withdraws its support, the Chief

Minister shall table a resolution in the Provincial Assembly for a vote of confidence
within thirty days.

### Clause (3)

(3) In case a resolution tabled pursuant to clauses (1) and (2) is not adopted by
a majority of the total number of then members of the Provincial Assembly, the Chief
Minister shall relieve of his or her office.

### Clause (4)

(4) One-fourth of the total number of then members of the Provincial
Assembly may table in writing a motion of no confidence against the Chief Minister.

#### Proviso-1

> Provided that a motion of no confidence may not be tabled until the first two
years after the appointment of the Chief Minister and until another one year after the
date of failure of the motion of no confidence once tabled.

### Clause (5)

(5) A motion of no confidence to be tabled pursuant to clause (4) shall also
mention the name of a member proposed for the Chief Minister.

### Clause (6)

(6) In case a motion of no confidence tabled pursuant to clause (4) is adopted
by a majority of the total number of then members of the Provincial Assembly, the
Chief Minister shall be relieved of his or her office.

### Clause (7)

(7) In case the office of the Chief Minister falls vacant because of the adoption
of a vote of no confidence pursuant to clause (6), the Chief of Province shall, pursuant
to Article 168, appoint as the Chief Minister the member of the Provincial Assembly
proposed pursuant to clause (5).
