# Article 196 -- Remuneration

> **Part 14:** Provincial Legislature  
> **Page:** 100

The remuneration and facilities of the Speaker, Deputy Speaker and
members of the Provincial Assembly shall be as provided for in the provincial law,
and until such law is made, as specified by the Provincial Government.
