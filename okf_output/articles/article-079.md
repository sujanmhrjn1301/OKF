# Article 79 -- Remuneration and other Facilities of Prime Minister and Ministers

> **Part 7:** Federal Executive  
> **Page:** 45

The remuneration
and other facilities of the Prime Minister and Ministers shall be as provided for in a
Federal Act. It shall be as specified by the Government of Nepal until such Act is
made.
