# Article 154 -- Judicial Service Commission

> **Part 11:** Judiciary  
> **Page:** 80

### Clause (1)

(1)The Government of Nepal shall, in appointing,
transferring or promoting gazetted officers of the Federal Judicial Service or taking
departmental action concerning such officers in accordance with law, act on
recommendation of the Judicial Service Commission.

#### Proviso-1

> Provided that the Government of Nepal shall consult the Public Service
Commission for new permanent appointments to gazetted posts of the Federal Judicial

Service from persons who are not already in the Federal Government Service or for
promotions from non-gazetted posts of the Federal Judicial Service to gazetted ones
of that Service.
Explanation: For the purposes of this Article, the Public Service Commission
shall hold open and internal competitive examinations to be held for appointments to
Gazetted posts of the Federal Judicial Service.

### Clause (2)

(2) The Judicial Service Commission shall consist of the following as its
chairperson and members:-

#### Sub-clause (2)(a)

(a)
The Chief Justice
- Chairperson

#### Sub-clause (2)(b)

(b)
The Federal Minister for Law and Justice
- Member

#### Sub-clause (2)(c)

(c)
The senior most Justice of Supreme Court
- Member

#### Sub-clause (2)(d)

(d)
The Chairperson of the Public Service
Commission
- Member

#### Sub-clause (2)(e)

(e)
The Attorney General
- Member

### Clause (3)

(3) Other functions, duties, powers and procedures of the Judicial Service
Commission shall be as provided for in the federal law.
