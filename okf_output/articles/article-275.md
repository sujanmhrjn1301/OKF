# Article 275 -- Provisions relating to Referendum

> **Part 32:** Miscellaneous  
> **Page:** 164

### Clause (1)

(1) In case a decision is made by a two-thirds
majority of the total number of the then members of the Federal Parliament that it is
necessary to hold a referendum with respect to any matter of national importance, a
decision on that matter may be taken by way of referendum.

### Clause (2)

(2) Matters relating to referendum and other relevant matters shall be as
provided for in the federal law.
