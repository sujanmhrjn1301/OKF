# Article 297 -- Provisions relating to President and Vice-President

> **Part 33:** Transitional Provisions  
> **Page:** 174

### Clause (1)

(1) The President and the Vice-
President existing at the time of commencement of this Constitution shall continue to
hold their respective offices until other President and Vice-President are elected
pursuant to this Article.

### Clause (2)

(2) The Legislature-Parliament set forth in clause (1) of Article 296 shall, on
the basis of political understanding, elect the President and the Vice-President, within
one month after the date of the commencement of this Constitution where the
Legislature-Parliament is not in recess at the time of commencement of this
Constitution and after the date on which the session is summoned pursuant to clause

### Clause (6)

(6) of Article 296 where the Legislature-Parliament is in recess.

### Clause (3)

(3) In the event of failure to have an understanding pursuant to clause (2), the
President and the Vice-President shall be elected by a majority of all the then
members of the Legislature-Parliament.

### Clause (4)

(4) In case the office of the President or Vice-President elected pursuant to
clause (2) or (3) falls vacant for any reason, the President or Vice-President shall be
elected by the Legislature-Parliament pursuant to this Article until the Federal
Parliament is constituted.

### Clause (5)

(5) The term of office of the President or Vice-President elected pursuant to
this Article shall continue to exist until another President or Vice-President elected by
an electoral college set forth in Article 62 assumes office.

### Clause (6)

(6) The office of the President or Vice-President elected pursuant to this
Article shall fall vacant in any of the following circumstances: -

#### Sub-clause (6)(a)

(a)
In case the President tenders resignation in writing to the Vice-
President and the Vice-President, to the President;

#### Sub-clause (6)(b)

(b)
In case a motion of impeachment against him or her is adopted
pursuant to clause (7);

#### Sub-clause (6)(c)

(c)
In case another President or Vice-President elected by an electoral
college set forth in Article 62 assumes office;

#### Sub-clause (6)(d)

(d)
In case he or she dies.

### Clause (7)

(7) At least one fourth of the total number of the then members of the
Legislature-Parliament referred to in clause (1) of Article 296 may move a motion of
impeachment against the President or Vice-President elected pursuant to this Article
on the ground of serious violation of this Constitution and law. In case the motion is
adopted by at least two-thirds majority of the total number of the then members of the
Legislature-Parliament, he or she shall be relieved of his or her office
