# Article 208 -- Provincial Appropriation Act

> **Part 16:** Province Financial Procedures  
> **Page:** 106

The moneys required to meet the expenditure to be
provided for by the Provincial Appropriation Act shall be specified under appropriate
heads in an Appropriation Bill.
