# Article 61 -- President

> **Part 6:** President and Vice-President  
> **Page:** 38

### Clause (1)

(1) There shall be a President of Nepal.

### Clause (2)

(2) The President shall be the Head of State of Nepal. He or she shall perform
his or her functions in accordance with this Constitution and federal law.

### Clause (3)

(3) The President shall promote national unity of Nepal.

### Clause (4)

(4) The main duty of the President shall be to abide by and protect the
Constitution.
