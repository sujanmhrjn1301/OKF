# Article 192 -- Transaction of Business in Case of Vacancy in Seat of Member

> **Part 14:** Provincial Legislature  
> **Page:** 100

The Provincial
Assembly shall have the power to transact its business notwithstanding any vacancy
in the seat of its member; and no proceedings of the Provincial Assembly already
conducted shall become invalid even in case it is subsequently discovered that a
person who was not so entitled took part in such proceedings.
