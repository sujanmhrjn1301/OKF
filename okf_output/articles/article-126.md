# Article 126 -- Courts to Exercise Powers relating to Justice

> **Part 11:** Judiciary  
> **Page:** 67

### Clause (1)

(1) Powers relating to justice in Nepal
shall be exercised by courts and other judicial institutions in accordance with this
Constitution, other laws and recognized principles of justice.

### Clause (2)

(2) All shall abide by the orders or decisions made by courts in the course of
hearing of lawsuit.
