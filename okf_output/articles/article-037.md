# Article 37 -- Right to Housing

> **Part 3:** Fundamental Rights and Duties  
> **Page:** 17

### Clause (1)

(1) Every citizen shall have the right to an appropriate housing.

### Clause (2)

(2) No citizen shall be evicted from the residence owned by him or her nor
shall his or her residence be infringed except in accordance with law.
