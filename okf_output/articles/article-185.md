# Article 185 -- Quorum of Provincial Assembly

> **Part 14:** Provincial Legislature  
> **Page:** 97

Except as otherwise provided for in this
Constitution, no question or motion shall be submitted for a decision in the Provincial
Assembly unless one-fourth of the total number of its members are present.
