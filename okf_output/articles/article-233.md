# Article 233 -- Relations between Provinces

> **Part 20:** Interrelations between Federation, Province and Local Level  
> **Page:** 120

### Clause (1)

(1) One Province shall render assistance in the
execution of legal provisions or judicial and administrative decisions or orders of
another Province.

### Clause (2)

(2) A Province may exchange information and consult with another Province
on matters of common concern and interest, coordinate each other on their activities
and legislations and extend mutual assistance.

### Clause (3)

(3) A Province shall, in accordance with its provincial law, provide equal
security, treatment and facility to residents of another Province.
