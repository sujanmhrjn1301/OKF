# Article 268 -- Provisions Relating to Nepal Police, Armed Police Force, Nepal and National Investigation Department

> **Part 28:** Provisions Relating to National Security  
> **Page:** 157

### Clause (1)

(1) The Federation shall have Nepal Police, Armed Police
Force, Nepal and National Investigation Department.

### Clause (2)

(2) Each Province shall have a Provincial Police Organization.

### Clause (3)

(3) Matters relating to the operation, supervision and coordination of functions
to be discharged by the Nepal Police and the Provincial police shall be as provided for
in federal law.

### Clause (4)

(4) Other matters relating to the Nepal Police, Armed Police Force, Nepal and
National Investigation Department shall be as provided for in the by federal law.
