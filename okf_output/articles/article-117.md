# Article 117 -- Expenditures from Federal Consolidated Fund or Federal Government Fund

> **Part 10:** Federal Financial Procedures  
> **Page:** 63

#### Sub-clause (a)

(a)
Moneys charged on the Federal Consolidated Fund;

#### Sub-clause (b)

(b)
Moneys required to meet the expenditure under a Federal
Appropriation Act;

#### Sub-clause (c)

(c)
Advance moneys authorized by a Federal Act required to meet
expenditures, when an Appropriation Bill is under consideration; or

#### Sub-clause (d)

(d)
Expenditures to be incurred in extraordinary circumstances under a
Federal Vote of Credit Act, which contains only a description of
expenditures.

#### Proviso-1

> Provided that matters relating to the Federal Contingency Fund
shall be governed by Article 124.
