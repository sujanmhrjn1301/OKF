# Article 205 -- Expenditures from Provincial Consolidated Fund or Provincial Government Fund

> **Part 16:** Province Financial Procedures  
> **Page:** 105

#### Sub-clause (a)

(a)
Moneys charged on the Provincial Consolidated Fund;

#### Sub-clause (b)

(b)
Moneys required to meet the expenditure under an Appropriation Act;

#### Sub-clause (c)

(c)
Advance moneys authorized by an Act required to meet expenditures,
when an Appropriation Bill is under consideration; or

#### Sub-clause (d)

(d)
Expenditures to be incurred in extraordinary circumstances under a
Vote of Credit Act which contains only a description of expenditures.

#### Proviso-1

> Provided that matters relating to the Provincial Contingency Fund shall
be pursuant to Article 212.
