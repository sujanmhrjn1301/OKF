# Article 244 -- Provisions relating to Provincial Public Service Commission

> **Part 23:** Public Service Commission  
> **Page:** 131

### Clause (1)

(1) Each Province shall
have a Provincial Public Service Commission.

### Clause (2)

(2) The functions, duties and powers of the Provincial Public Service
Commission shall be as provided for in the provincial law.

### Clause (3)

(3) The Federal Parliament shall by a law determine grounds and standards for
the purposes of clause (2).
