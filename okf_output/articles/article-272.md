# Article 272 -- Other Provisions relating to Political Parties

> **Part 29:** Provisions Relating to Political Parties  
> **Page:** 159

Provisions relating to the formation,
registration, operation and facilities of political parties and other matters pertaining
thereto shall be as provided for in the federal law.
