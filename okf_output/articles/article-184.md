# Article 184 -- Address by Chief of Province

> **Part 14:** Provincial Legislature  
> **Page:** 97

### Clause (1)

(1) The Chief of Province may address a sitting of the
Provincial Assembly and summon the members for that purpose.

### Clause (2)

(2) The Chief of Province shall address the first session after an election to the
Provincial Assembly and a sitting of the Provincial Assembly after the
commencement of the first session of each year.
