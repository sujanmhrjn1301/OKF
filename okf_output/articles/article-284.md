# Article 284 -- Provisions relating to Constitutional Council

> **Part 32:** Miscellaneous  
> **Page:** 166

### Clause (1)

(1) There shall be a Constitutional
Council for making recommendations for appointment of the Chief Justice and Chiefs
and officials of the Constitutional Bodies in accordance with this Constitution, which
shall consist of the following as the Chairperson and members:-

#### Sub-clause (1)(a)

(a)
The Prime Minister
- Chairperson

#### Sub-clause (1)(b)

(b)
The Chief Justice
- Member

#### Sub-clause (1)(c)

(c)
The Speaker of the House of Representatives
- Member

#### Sub-clause (1)(d)

(d)
The Chairperson of the National Assembly
 - Member

#### Sub-clause (1)(e)

(e)
Leader of Opposition Party in the House of Representatives  - Member

#### Sub-clause (1)(f)

(f)
Deputy Speaker of the House of Representatives
- Member

### Clause (2)

(2) While making a recommendation for appointment to the office of the Chief
Justice, the Constitutional Council shall include the Minister for Law and Justice of
the Government of Nepal, as its member.

### Clause (3)

(3) The Constitutional Council shall make a recommendation for appointment
under this Constitution before one month of the vacation of the office of the Chief
Justice or a chief or official of a Constitutional Body.

#### Proviso-1

> Provided that in case such office falls vacant because of death or resignation, it
may so make recommendation for appointment that the office is fulfilled within one
month after the date of vacancy.

### Clause (4)

(4) Other functions, duties and powers of the Constitutional Council and rules
of procedures on the appointment of the Chief Justice or chiefs or officials of
Constitutional Bodies shall be as provided for in the federal law.

### Clause (5)

(5) The Chief Secretary to the Government of Nepal shall act as the secretary
to the Constitutional Council.
