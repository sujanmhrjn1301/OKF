# Article 230 -- Estimates of Revenues and Expenditures of Rural Municipality and Municipality

> **Part 19:** Local Financial Procedures  
> **Page:** 118

### Clause (1)

(1)
The Rural Municipal Executive and the Municipal Executive shall, subject to this
Constitution, lay an estimate of revenues and expenditures of every fiscal year before, and
have the estimate adopted by, the Rural Municipal Assembly and the Municipal
Assembly, respectively, as provided for in the Local law.

### Clause (2)

(2) In case, in laying an estimate of revenues and expenditures pursuant to
clause (1), the Rural Municipal Executive or Municipal Executive is required to make
a deficit budget, it shall also propose the sources to meet the deficit as provided for in
the federal law and provincial law.
