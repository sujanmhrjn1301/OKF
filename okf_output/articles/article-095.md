# Article 95 -- Address by President

> **Part 8:** Federal Legislature  
> **Page:** 52

### Clause (1)

(1) The President may address either a meeting of any House or
a joint sitting of both Houses of the Federal Parliament, and may summon the
members for that purpose.

### Clause (2)

(2) The President shall address the first session after election to the House of
Representatives and a joint sitting of both Houses of the Federal Parliament after the
commencement of the first session of each year.
