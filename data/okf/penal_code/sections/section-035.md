# Section 35: Additional claim can be made

**Act:** The National Criminal Procedure (Code) Act, 2017  
**Chapter 3:** Prosecution and Filing of Cases  
*Page 51*

---

If, based on the other relevant 
additional evidence found after a case has already been filed in the 
court, it appears necessary to make a charge also against other 
accused in that case, in addition to the accused against whom the 
case has already been filed, or to make additional charge against the 
person who has already been made accused upon the filing of the 
case, the government attorney may, setting out the reasons therefor, 
cause further investigation to be conducted, and file a case, making 
additional claim against the other additional accused also.
