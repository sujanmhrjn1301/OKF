# Section 146: Appeal against reference judgment

**Act:** The National Criminal Procedure (Code) Act, 2017  
**Chapter 14:** Provisions Relating to Appeal and Reference for Sanction  
*Page 153*

---

A party, who is dissatisfied 
with the judgment so passed by the court as to be referred to for 
sanction pursuant to Section 145, may appeal against it.
