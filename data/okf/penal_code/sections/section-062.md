# Section 62: Summons may be served through print or electronic 
communication media

**Act:** The National Criminal Procedure (Code) Act, 2017  
**Chapter 6:** Warrant for Arrest and Summons  
*Page 80*

---

### Sub-section (1)
If any summons cannot be served 
pursuant to Section 61, the court may serve the summons by 
publishing the contents of such summons in such national daily 
newspaper or broadcasting through electronic communication media 
as owned by the Government of Nepal.

### Sub-section (2)
If a summons is published or broadcast pursuant to sub-
section (1), the summons shall be deemed to have been duly served.
