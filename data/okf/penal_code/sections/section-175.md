# Section 175: Copies to be provided

**Act:** The National Criminal Procedure (Code) Act, 2017  
**Chapter 16:** Miscellaneous  
*Page 182*

---

### Sub-section (1)
The concerned party to a case or his or 
her law practitioner may obtain a copy of any document contained in 
the case-file of any case, by paying such fees as provided by law.

> **Provided that any law practitioner may obtain the copy of a 
judgment.**

### Sub-section (2)
Notwithstanding anything contained in sub-section (1), 
no copy of a document that has to be kept confidential pursuant to 
law shall be provided.
