# Section 45: Offences to be tried by court having jurisdiction over area where 
offences committed

**Act:** The National Criminal Procedure (Code) Act, 2017  
**Chapter 4:** Jurisdiction of Court  
*Page 57*

---

The power to proceed and adjudicate any case 
related to any offence shall be vested in the court having jurisdiction 
over the area where such offence is committed. 
Provided that the Government of Nepal may, in accordance 
with law, constitute a separate criminal court or criminal bench in 
any district for the proceeding and adjudication of such cases.

> **Provided that the Government of Nepal may, in accordance 
with law, constitute a separate criminal court or criminal bench in 
any district for the proceeding and adjudication of such cases.**
