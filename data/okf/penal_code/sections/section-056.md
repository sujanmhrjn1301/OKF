# Section 56: Government of Nepal to be made as plaintiff

**Act:** The National Criminal Procedure (Code) Act, 2017  
**Chapter 5:** Provisions Relating to Preliminary Proceedings of Cases  
*Page 67*

---

A case related to 
any of the offences under Schedule-1 and Schedule 2, other than a 
case instituted upon a complaint referred to in sub-section (2) of 
Section 52, shall be proceeded by making the Government of Nepal 
as the plaintiff.
