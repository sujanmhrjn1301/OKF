# Section 41: Notice to be given to informants or victims

**Act:** The National Criminal Procedure (Code) Act, 2017  
**Chapter 3:** Prosecution and Filing of Cases  
*Page 54*

---

If decision is made 
not to institute a case in connection with any offence pursuant to sub-
section (3) of Section 31, the investigating authority shall, as soon as 
possible, give information thereof, accompanied by the reason 
therefor, to the informant or victim.
