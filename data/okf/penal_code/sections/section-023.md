# Section 23: Expert opinion may be obtained

**Act:** The National Criminal Procedure (Code) Act, 2017  
**Chapter 2:** Provisions Relating to Information on Commission of, and Investigation into, Offences  
*Page 34*

---

### Sub-section (1)
The investigating authority 
may, if he or she so deems necessary, be accompanied by any expert 
in the course of investigation or seek opinion of any expert on any 
matter connected with the offence.

### Sub-section (2)
It shall be the duty of the concerned expert to give his or 
her opinion if he or she is so asked pursuant to sub-section (1).

### Sub-section (3)
The expert opinion given pursuant to sub-section (2) 
shall be recorded in the case-file.

### Sub-section (4)
If any expert referred to in this Section is a non-
governmental person, the Government of Nepal shall provide 
expenses to be incurred in the obtaining of his or her service.

### Sub-section (5)
Notwithstanding anything contained elsewhere in this 
Section, if the investigating authority, for the reason of the sensitivity 
or complexity of any case, considers appropriate to do so, and makes 
recommendation for obtaining the opinion of the medical board or 
any other expert in such case, the Government of Nepal may form 
such board.

### Sub-section (6)
If a board is formed pursuant to sub-section (5), the 
board shall give opinion on that matter.
