# Section 54: Cases can be instituted together

**Act:** The National Criminal Procedure (Code) Act, 2017  
**Chapter 5:** Provisions Relating to Preliminary Proceedings of Cases  
*Page 66*

---

### Sub-section (1)
A case may be instituted 
against the following persons upon the same charge sheet, report, 
complaint, plaint or memorandum of order:

- **Clause (a)**: Persons who commit the same offence or different 
offences on the same occasion,
- **Clause (b)**: Persons who commit one or more than one offence 
jointly or in connivance with each other,
- **Clause (c)**: Persons who commit various offences related to each 
other.

### Sub-section (2)
In the case of the persons involved in the following 
offence, a case may be instituted upon the same charge sheet, report, 
complaint, plaint or memorandum of order:

- **Clause (a)**: Attempt, criminal conspiracy to commit, or abetment of, 
the offence,
- **Clause (b)**: Accomplice to the offence,
- **Clause (c)**: Offence of possessing, buying, selling, giving or taking 
any thing acquired from the offence,
- **Clause (d)**: Offence of possessing, buying, selling, giving or taking 
any thing with the intention of committing the offence, or
- **Clause (e)**: Offence of hiding any person connected with the 
offence or aiding such person to abscond.
