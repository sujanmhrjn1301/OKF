# Section 55: Separate complaint to be made in relation to each offence or 
accused

**Act:** The National Criminal Procedure (Code) Act, 2017  
**Chapter 5:** Provisions Relating to Preliminary Proceedings of Cases  
*Page 67*

---

Except as otherwise provided in Sections 53 and 54, a 
separate charge sheet, complaint or memorandum of order shall be 
framed or made in relation to each offence or accused.
