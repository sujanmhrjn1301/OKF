# Section 92: Powers and duties of attorney

**Act:** The National Criminal Procedure (Code) Act, 2017  
**Chapter 10:** Provisions Relating to Transfer and Adjournment of Cases  
*Page 104*

---

### Sub-section (1)
An attorney may carry out any 
acts set forth in the power of attorney on behalf of the party 
appointing him or her.

### Sub-section (2)
If the court makes order to cause the appearance of the 
person appointing attorney in person, the attorney shall cause such 
person to make appearance in the court.

### Sub-section (3)
If the concerned person fails to appear pursuant to sub-
section (2), the court may void the attorney of such person.
