# Section 90: Person may become attorney of more than one person 
simultaneously

**Act:** The National Criminal Procedure (Code) Act, 2017  
**Chapter 10:** Provisions Relating to Transfer and Adjournment of Cases  
*Page 103*

---

A person may become an attorney of more than one 
person simultaneously. 
Provided that a person who is an attorney of a party to a case 
shall not also become an attorney of the opposite party to the same 
case or a case related to that case.

> **Provided that a person who is an attorney of a party to a case 
shall not also become an attorney of the opposite party to the same 
case or a case related to that case.**
