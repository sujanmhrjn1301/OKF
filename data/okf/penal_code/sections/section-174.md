# Section 174: Assistance of interpreter may be taken

**Act:** The National Criminal Procedure (Code) Act, 2017  
**Chapter 16:** Miscellaneous  
*Page 181*

---

### Sub-section (1)
If it is required to take 
statement or deposition of any accused or witness who is unable to 
understand or speak the Nepali language or for any other purpose, 
the investigating authority, prosecutor or the court may take 
assistance of an interpreter of the concerned language or the sign 
language.

### Sub-section (2)
The Government of Nepal shall bear the expenses 
incurred in taking assistance of the interpreter pursuant to sub-
section (1).
