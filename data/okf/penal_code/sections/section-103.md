# Section 103: Cross examination of the co-accused

**Act:** The National Criminal Procedure (Code) Act, 2017  
**Chapter 11:** Provisions Relating to Examination of Evidence  
*Page 115*

---

If, in examining evidence 
pursuant to this Chapter, any co-accused makes accusation against 
another co-accused, such co-accused may make cross-examination to 
the extent of such accusation.
