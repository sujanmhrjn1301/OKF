# Section 138: Calling for case file

**Act:** The National Criminal Procedure (Code) Act, 2017  
**Chapter 14:** Provisions Relating to Appeal and Reference for Sanction  
*Page 149*

---

### Sub-section (1)
Except in cases where the case-file is 
received pursuant to sub-section (3) of Section 135, the appeal 
hearing court shall, upon the filing of an appeal, and as soon as 
possible, order the concerned court to send the case-file of such case.

### Sub-section (2)
The concerned court shall send the case-file to the 
appeal hearing court within three days from the date of receipt of the 
order pursuant to sub-section (1).
