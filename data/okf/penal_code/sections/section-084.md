# Section 84: Appointment of date for appearance to be made in a manner to 
be convenient to parties

**Act:** The National Criminal Procedure (Code) Act, 2017  
**Chapter 9:** Provisions Relating to Attorney  
*Page 97*

---

### Sub-section (1)
While appointing a date for 
appearance for any purpose in a case, the court shall appoint such 
date, as far as practicable, taking into consideration of whether it 
would be convenient to the parties to the case and the specified 
purpose could be accomplished or not.

### Sub-section (2)
While appointing a date for appearance pursuant to sub-
section (1), it shall be appointed in accordance with the schedule of 
business specified in relation to the proceedings of the case.
