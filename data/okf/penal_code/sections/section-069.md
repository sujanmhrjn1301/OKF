# Section 69: To remand on recognizance

**Act:** The National Criminal Procedure (Code) Act, 2017  
**Chapter 7:** Provisions Relating to Detention, Bail and Guarantee  
*Page 90*

---

The case shall be tried by remanding 
the accused on recognizance to appear on appointed days when the 
accused appears in the court, in any case other than that referred to in 
Sections 67 and 68.
