# Section 186: Receipt to be taken or given

**Act:** The National Criminal Procedure (Code) Act, 2017  
**Chapter 16:** Miscellaneous  
*Page 190*

---

### Sub-section (1)
While returning any document 
registered in the court to any person or handing over any document 
by the court to any person, a receipt or proof to the effect that such 
document has been so returned or handed over shall be executed or 
taken and enclosed in the case-file.

### Sub-section (2)
The court shall, while receiving any document 
submitted by any person to it, give a receipt thereof to that person.
