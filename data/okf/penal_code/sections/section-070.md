# Section 70: Additional bail/bond, guarantee or bank guarantee may be 
demanded

**Act:** The National Criminal Procedure (Code) Act, 2017  
**Chapter 7:** Provisions Relating to Detention, Bail and Guarantee  
*Page 90*

---

### Sub-section (1)
If it appears subsequently that the bail/bond, 
guarantee or bank guarantee taken from any accused is insufficient, 
the court may demand additional bail/bond, guarantee or bank 
guarantee from the accused.

### Sub-section (2)
If the accused fails to furnish such additional bail/bond, 
guarantee or bank guarantee referred to in sub-section (1), the court 
may remand such accused in detention.
