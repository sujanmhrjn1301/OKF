# Section 72: Grounds for fixation of amount of bail/bond, guarantee or bank 
guarantee

**Act:** The National Criminal Procedure (Code) Act, 2017  
**Chapter 7:** Provisions Relating to Detention, Bail and Guarantee  
*Page 91*

---

### Sub-section (1)
The amount of a bail/bond, guarantee or bank 
guarantee to be taken pursuant to this Chapter shall be fixed 
reasonably, having regard to the following matters:

- **Clause (a)**: Nature and gravity of the offence,
- **Clause (b)**: Financial status and family condition of the 
accused or the offender,
- **Clause (c)**: Age and physical condition of the accused or the 
offender,
- **Clause (d)**: Whether he or she was previously convicted of 
any offence and sentenced for the same or not,
- **Clause (e)**: Whether he or she has committed several 
offences on the same occasion or not,
- **Clause (f)**: Sentence imposed or imposable on him or her, 
and compensation to be borne by him or her,
- **Clause (g)**: Consequences resulted from the offence,
- **Clause (h)**: Whether he or she has pleaded guilty of the 
offence or not,
- **Clause (i)**: Where the accused is a helpless or infirm person 
or pregnant woman or woman accompanying a 
baby whom she breast feeds.

### Sub-section (2)
If any party to a case makes a petition to the appeal 
hearing court showing the reason that the amount of a bail/bond, 
guarantee or bank guarantee demanded from any person under this 
Act is lesser or excessive and is not thus reasonable, such court may 
hear the matter and order the alteration or modification in the amount 
of such bail/bond, guarantee or bank guarantee.
