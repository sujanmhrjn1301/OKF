# Section 196: Power to frame rules

**Act:** The National Criminal Procedure (Code) Act, 2017  
**Chapter 16:** Miscellaneous  
*Page 194*

---

### Sub-section (1)
The Government of Nepal may, in 
consultation with the Attorney General, frame rules on matters of 
investigation, and the Supreme Court may frame rules on matters of 
court proceedings, for the implementation of this Act.

### Sub-section (2)
While framing rules pursuant to sub-section (1), the 
Judicial Council shall be consulted on the matters pertaining to the 
administration of justice.
