# Section 79: Memorandum of order to be executed

**Act:** The National Criminal Procedure (Code) Act, 2017  
**Chapter 8:** Provisions Relating to Appointed Date  
*Page 95*

---

While remanding any 
accused in detention, releasing any accused held in detention or 
taking a bail/bond or guarantee from any accused or modifying or 
altering such bail/bond or guarantee pursuant to this Chapter or 
releasing from detention pursuant to Section 77, the court shall 
execute a memorandum of order to that effect, accompanied by the 
reason for the same.
