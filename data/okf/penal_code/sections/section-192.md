# Section 192: Documents may be exchanged through electronic means

**Act:** The National Criminal Procedure (Code) Act, 2017  
**Chapter 16:** Miscellaneous  
*Page 193*

---

The 
documents referred to in this Act may be exchanged through 
electronic means in accordance with the law in force related to 
electronic transactions.
