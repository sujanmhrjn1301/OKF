# Section 132: Notice and copy of judgment to be given

**Act:** The National Criminal Procedure (Code) Act, 2017  
**Chapter 13:** Provisions Relating to Proceedings, Hearing of, and Judgments in, Cases  
*Page 141*

---

### Sub-section (1)
The court shall give a 
notice of judgment in the form referred to in Schedule-44 to the 
accused or defendant who has not been involved in the proceedings 
of the case from the beginning, the accused held in detention or 
imprisonment or the accused of a case heard ex parte.

### Sub-section (2)
The notice referred to in sub-section (1) shall be served 
as if it were a summons, and if such notice cannot be served, it shall 
be published in any national daily newspaper owned by the 
Government of Nepal.

### Sub-section (3)
The concerned party shall bear the expenses to be 
incurred in the publication of a notice referred to in sub-section (2) in 
relation to any case under Schedule-4.

### Sub-section (4)
The information of judgment of any of the cases under 
Schedule-1, Schedule-2 and Schedule-3 shall also be given to the 
concerned government attorney office.
