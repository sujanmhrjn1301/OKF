# Section 93: Revocation of attorney

**Act:** The National Criminal Procedure (Code) Act, 2017  
**Chapter 10:** Provisions Relating to Transfer and Adjournment of Cases  
*Page 104*

---

### Sub-section (1)
If a party who has appointed an attorney 
wishes to revoke the power of attorney executed by him or her, the 
party may, at any time, revoke the power of attorney and carry out 
acts pertaining to the case in person or appoint another person as his 
or her attorney.

### Sub-section (2)
In the event of the revocation of the power of attorney 
and carrying out of acts pertaining to the case by a party in person, 

 
 
the concerned party shall give information thereof through petition to 
the court.
