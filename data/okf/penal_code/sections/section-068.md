# Section 68: Bail /bond, guarantee or bank guarantee to be taken from the 
accused

**Act:** The National Criminal Procedure (Code) Act, 2017  
**Chapter 7:** Provisions Relating to Detention, Bail and Guarantee  
*Page 89*

---

### Sub-section (1)
Except in the case referred to in Section 67, the court 
may ask for a bail/bond, guarantee or bank guarantee from the 
accused if there exists a reasonable ground for proving the charge 
against the accused.

> **Provided that the bank guarantee shall be unconditional and 
renewable at such times as may be specified by the court.**

### Sub-section (2)
The court shall remand in detention any accused who 
fails to furnish bail/bond, guarantee or bank guarantee pursuant to 
sub-section (1).
