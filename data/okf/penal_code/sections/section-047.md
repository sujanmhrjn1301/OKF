# Section 47: Jurisdiction to try offences committed outside Nepal

**Act:** The National Criminal Procedure (Code) Act, 2017  
**Chapter 5:** Provisions Relating to Preliminary Proceedings of Cases  
*Page 59*

---

If the law in 
force provides for punishment in respect of any offence committed 
outside Nepal or on an aircraft or ship registered in Nepal, such 
offence shall be proceeded and adjudicated by the court of the 
district where the person accused of such offence is found and by the 
District Court of the area where such accused has permanent abode, 
if such accused is not found.
