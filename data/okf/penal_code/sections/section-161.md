# Section 161: Period of detention undergone to be set off against sentence of 
imprisonment

**Act:** The National Criminal Procedure (Code) Act, 2017  
**Chapter 15:** Provisions Relating to Execution of Judgments  
*Page 167*

---

### Sub-section (1)
If a person who, on conviction, is sentenced to 
imprisonment by a court judgment, has remained in detention or 
custody in the course of investigation, the period of such detention or 
custody shall be set off against the term of imprisonment imposed on 
him or her by the judgment, and such offender shall be imprisoned 
only for the remaining term of imprisonment.

### Sub-section (2)
If any offender who is not sentenced to imprisonment 
but to a fine only has remained in detention or custody in the course 
of investigation, the total amount to be set by three hundred rupees a 
day of the period of his or her detention or custody shall be set off 
against the amount of fine payable by him or her.
