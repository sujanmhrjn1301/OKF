# Section 83: Sheet of appointed date for appearance

**Act:** The National Criminal Procedure (Code) Act, 2017  
**Chapter 9:** Provisions Relating to Attorney  
*Page 97*

---

### Sub-section (1)
The court shall give a 
sheet of appointed date for appearance to the parties to each case in 
the form referred to in Schedule-35.

### Sub-section (2)
The concerned employee shall state the date appointed for 
appearance and the purpose for which that date is so appointed in the 
sheet referred to in sub-section (1), and sign it.
