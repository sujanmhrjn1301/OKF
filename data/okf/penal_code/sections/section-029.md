# Section 29: Power to forward the matter to the concerned body

**Act:** The National Criminal Procedure (Code) Act, 2017  
**Chapter 2:** Provisions Relating to Information on Commission of, and Investigation into, Offences  
*Page 38*

---

Notwithstanding anything contained elsewhere in this Act, if, in the 
course of investigating into any offence under Schedule-1 or 
Schedule-2, it appears that the offence has to be investigated into 

 
 
under other law, the matter, accompanied by the progress details of 
investigation, may be forwarded to the concerned body or authority 
for investigation into it.
