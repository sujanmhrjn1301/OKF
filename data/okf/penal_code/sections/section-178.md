# Section 178: Procedure to be followed for releasing detainee or prisoner

**Act:** The National Criminal Procedure (Code) Act, 2017  
**Chapter 16:** Miscellaneous  
*Page 185*

---

### Sub-section (1)
If 
any detainee or prisoner has to be released pursuant to the judgment 
or order of the court, the court shall send such order stating the 
reasons for release, full name and address of the detainee or prisoner 
and detail of the case to the concerned prison.

### Sub-section (2)
Upon receipt of the order referred to in sub-section (1), 
the concerned prison shall immediately release the detainee or 
prisoner in pursuance of such order.

### Sub-section (3)
Notwithstanding anything contained in sub-sections (1) 
and (2), if any detainee or prisoner to be released pursuant to the 

 
 
judgment or order is present in the court, he or she shall, upon 
judgment, be released immediately and a notice thereof shall be 
given to the concerned prison.
