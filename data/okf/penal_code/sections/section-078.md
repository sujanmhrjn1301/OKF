# Section 78: Maximum allowable period of detention

**Act:** The National Criminal Procedure (Code) Act, 2017  
**Chapter 8:** Provisions Relating to Appointed Date  
*Page 95*

---

Notwithstanding 
anything contained elsewhere in this Chapter, no accused shall be 
held in detention for a period exceeding the maximum term of the 
sentence of imprisonment than can be imposed on him or her if the 
charge made against him or her is proved.
