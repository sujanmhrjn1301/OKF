# Section 119: Consequences of withdrawal or compromise of cases

**Act:** The National Criminal Procedure (Code) Act, 2017  
**Chapter 12:** Provisions Relating to Withdrawal, Compromise and Mediation of Cases  
*Page 128*

---

### Sub-section (1)
If the 
Government of Nepal withdraws any case pursuant to Section 116, 
no case shall be instituted again against the accused of that case in 
respect of the same offence.

### Sub-section (2)
If the compromise of a case is made pursuant to Section 
117, no action or case shall be capable of being instituted 
subsequently between the same parties in respect of the same matter.

> **Provided that nothing contained in this sub-section shall bar 
the making of a petition claiming that any party has not complied 
with the terms contained in the deed of compromise.**
