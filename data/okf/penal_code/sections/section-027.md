# Section 27: Power of the Attorney General to give direction in the course of 
investigation

**Act:** The National Criminal Procedure (Code) Act, 2017  
**Chapter 2:** Provisions Relating to Information on Commission of, and Investigation into, Offences  
*Page 37*

---

### Sub-section (1)
The Attorney General may obtain necessary 

 
 
information about the investigation undergoing into any criminal 
case to be filed by the Government of Nepal.

### Sub-section (2)
If, upon having information pursuant to sub-section (1), 
the Attorney General is of the opinion that investigation into any 
criminal offence which is a state case is insufficient or that any 
special measure, method or mode required to be adopted or followed 
have not been adopted or followed during investigation into such 
offence or that further investigation should be conducted into such 
offence, he or she may, at any time, give direction to the 
investigating authority or the departmental head of that authority in 
respect of the investigation.

### Sub-section (3)
It shall be the duty of the concerned authority to comply 
with the direction given pursuant to sub-section (2).
