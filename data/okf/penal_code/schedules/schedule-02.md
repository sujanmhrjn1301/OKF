# Schedule 2: (Relating to clauses (d) and (l) of Section 2)

**Act:** The National Criminal Procedure (Code) Act, 2017  

*Page 199*

---

1. 
Offences under the following Sections of the following Chapter of 
the Penal Code: 
 
(a) 
Sections 107, 108, 109, 111, 112 and 113 of Chapter-5 
of Part-2 
2. 
Any criminal case, which is instituted with the Government of Nepal 
being plaintiff, or any offence of criminal nature in which the right, 
interest or concern of the Government of Nepal is vested, except that 
mentioned in Schedule-1. 
3. 
Such offence as may be included by the law in force in this 
Schedule. 
4. 
Such offence as may be specified to be tried and adjudicated under 
this Schedule, by the Government of Nepal, by a notification in the 
Nepal Gazette. 
5. 
Offence of attempt to, abetment of, conspiracy to, any offence under 
numbers 1, 2, 3 and 4, and any offence related thereto under Chapter 
11.
