**Act:** The National Criminal Procedure (Code) Act, 2017  

*Page 200*

---

1. 
Offences under Chapter-3 of Part-2 of the Penal Code. 
2. 
Any criminal offence in which the Government of Nepal is 
concerned or in which the right, interest or concern of any 
constitutional body, court or public authority is vested, other than 
that set forth in Schedule-1 or Schedule-2.
