## Full Text



Part-18 
Local Legislature 
221. 
Legislative Powers of Local Level: (1) Subject to this Constitution, the legislative 
powers of the Local Level shall be vested in the Rural Municipal Assembly and the 
Municipal Assembly. 
 
 
(2) The legislative powers of the Rural Municipal Assembly and the 
Municipal Assembly shall be as enumerated in the lists contained in Schedule-8 and 
Schedule-9. 
222. 
Composition of Rural Municipal Assembly: (1) There shall be a Rural Municipal 
Assembly in each Rural Municipality. 
(2) A Rural Municipal Assembly referred to in clause (1) shall consist of the 
Chairperson and Vice-Chairperson of the Rural Municipal Executive, Ward 
Chairpersons, and four members elected from each ward and Members of the Rural 
Municipal Executive elected from Dalit and minority communities pursuant to clause 
(4) of Article 215. 
(3) A Rural Municipal Assembly to be formed pursuant to clause (1) shall 
have representation of at least two women from each ward. 
(4) Each ward of a Rural Municipality shall have a Ward Committee 
composed of the Ward Chairperson and four members in accordance with the federal 
law. Such Ward Chairperson and Ward members shall be elected in accordance with 
the first past the post electoral system. 
(5) Every person who has completed the age of eighteen years and whose 
name is included in the electoral rolls of the Rural Municipality shall have a right to 
vote as provided for in the federal law. 
(6) A person who has the following qualifications shall be eligible to become a 
candidate for the office of the Member of the Rural Municipal Assembly:- 
(a) 
One who is a citizen of Nepal; 
(b) 
One who has completed the age of twenty-one years; 

(c) 
One whose name is included as a voter in the electoral rolls of the 
Rural Municipality; and 
(d) 
One who is not disqualified by any law. 
(7) Matters relating to the election to the Rural Municipal Assembly and other 
matters pertaining thereto shall be as provided for in the federal law. 
223. 
Composition of Municipal Assembly: (1) Each Municipality shall have a Municipal 
Assembly. 
(2) A Municipal Assembly referred to in clause (1) shall consist of the Mayor 
and the Deputy Mayor of the Municipal Executive, Ward Chairpersons, and four 
members elected from each Ward and members of the Municipal Executive elected 
from Dalit or minority communities pursuant to clause (4) of Article 216. 
(3) A Municipal Assembly to be formed pursuant to (1) shall have 
representation of at least two women from each Ward. 
(4) Each ward of a Municipality shall have a Ward Committee composed of 
one Ward Chairperson and four members as provided for in the federal law. Such 
Ward Chairperson and Ward members shall be elected in accordance with the first 
past the post electoral system. 
(5) Every person who has completed the age of eighteen years and whose 
name is included in the electoral rolls of the Municipality shall have a right to vote as 
provided for in federal law. 
(6) A person who has the following qualifications shall be qualified to be a 
candidate for the office of member of the Municipal Assembly:- 
(a) 
One who is a citizen of Nepal; 
(b) 
One who has completed the age of twenty-one years; 
(c) 
One whose name included in the electoral rolls of the Municipality; 
and 
(d) 
 One who is not disqualified by any law. 
(7) Matters relating to the election to the Municipal Assembly and other 
matters pertaining thereto shall be as provided for in the federal law. 

224. 
Chairperson and Vice-Chairperson of Rural Municipal Assembly and Municipal 
Assembly: The Chairperson and Vice-Chairperson of a Rural Municipal Executive 
and the Mayor and Deputy Mayor of a Municipal Executive shall ipso facto be the 
Chairperson and Vice-Chairperson of the Rural Municipal Assembly and the 
Municipal Assembly, respectively, and perform functions as such. 
225. 
Term of Rural Municipal Assembly and Municipal Assembly: The term of a Rural 
Municipal Assembly and of a Municipal Assembly shall be five years from the date of 
election. Election to another Rural Municipal Assembly and Municipal Assembly 
shall be completed within six months of the expiration of such term. 
226. 
Powers to Make Law: (1) A Rural Municipal Assembly and Municipal Assembly may 
make necessary laws on the matters enumerated in the lists contained in Schedule-8 
and Schedule-9. 
 
 
(2) The process for making laws referred to in clause (1) shall be as provided 
for in a provincial law.  
227. 
Other Matters relating to Rural Municipal Assembly and Municipal Assembly: Other 
matters relating to the conduct of business of a Rural Municipal Assembly and 
Municipal Assembly, rules of procedures of meetings, formation of committees, 
conditions in which the office of member falls vacant, facilities to be entitled to 
members of the Rural Municipal Assembly and Municipal Assembly and employees 
and offices of the Rural Municipality and Municipality, as the case may be, shall be as 
provided for in a provincial law.
