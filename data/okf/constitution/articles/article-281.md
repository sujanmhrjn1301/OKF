# Article 281 -- Appraisal and Review of Special Rights

> **Part 32:** Miscellaneous  
> **Page:** 165

The Government of Nepal shall make
appraisal and review of the implementation of special rights of the women and Dalit
community and impacts thereof, on the basis of human development index,
concurrently with a national census to be held in every ten years.
