# Article 213 -- Act relating to Financial Procedures

> **Part 16:** Province Financial Procedures  
> **Page:** 107

Matters relating to the transfer of moneys
appropriated by a provincial Act from one head to another and other financial
procedures shall be as provided for in the provincial Act.
