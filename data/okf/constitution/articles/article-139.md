# Article 139 -- High Courts

> **Part 11:** Judiciary  
> **Page:** 72

### Clause (1)

(1) There shall be a High Court in each Province.

### Clause (2)

(2) In case anyone makes obstruction in the dispensation of justice by, or
disregard any order or judgment handed down by, it or any of its subordinate courts or
judicial institutions, the High Court may initiate proceedings on and impose
punishment for contempt, as provided for in the federal law.

### Clause (3)

(3) Each High Court shall consist of such number of Judges, in addition to the
Chief Judge, as provided for in the federal law.
