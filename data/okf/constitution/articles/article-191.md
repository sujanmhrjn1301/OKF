# Article 191 -- Restriction on Discussion

> **Part 14:** Provincial Legislature  
> **Page:** 100

No discussion shall be held in the Provincial Assembly on
any matters that may cause adverse effect on the dispensation of justice on any cases
which are sub judice in any court of Nepal and on any judicial acts carried out by
Judges in the course of performance of their duties.
