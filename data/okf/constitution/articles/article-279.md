# Article 279 -- Ratification of, Accession to, Acceptance or Approval of, Treaties or Agreements

> **Part 32:** Miscellaneous  
> **Page:** 164

### Clause (1)

(1)
The ratification of, accession to, acceptance or approval of, treaties or agreements to
which Nepal or the Government of Nepal is to become a party shall be as provided for
in the federal law.

### Clause (2)

(2) Any law to be made pursuant to clause (1) shall, inter alia, require that the
ratification of, accession to, acceptance or approval of, treaties or agreements on the

following subjects shall be made by a majority of two-thirds of the total number of the
then members of both Houses of the Federal Parliament:-

#### Sub-clause (2)(a)

(a)
Peace and friendship;

#### Sub-clause (2)(b)

(b)
Defence and strategic alliance;

#### Sub-clause (2)(c)

(c)
Boundaries of the Nepal; and

#### Sub-clause (2)(d)

(d)
Natural resources, and the distribution of their uses.
Provided that, out of the treaties or agreements referred to in sub-clauses (a)
and (d), in case any treaty or agreement is of an ordinary nature, which does not affect
the nation extensively, seriously or in the long term, the ratification of, accession to,
acceptance or approval of, such treaty or agreement may be made by a simple
majority of the members present in a meeting of the House of Representatives.

### Clause (3)

(3) After the commencement of this Constitution, unless a treaty or agreement
is ratified, acceded to, accepted or approved pursuant to this Article, such treaty or
agreement shall not be binding to the Government of Nepal or Nepal.

### Clause (4)

(4) Notwithstanding anything contained in clauses (1) and (2), no treaty or
agreement that is detrimental to the territorial integrity of Nepal may be concluded.
