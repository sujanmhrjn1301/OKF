# Article 89 -- Vacation of Seat

> **Part 8:** Federal Legislature  
> **Page:** 49

#### Sub-clause (a)

(a)
In case he or she tenders resignation in writing to the Speaker or
Chairperson;

#### Sub-clause (b)

(b)
In case he or she is no longer qualified or ceases to possess the
qualification pursuant to Article 87;

#### Sub-clause (c)

(c)
In case the term of the House of Representatives or the term of office of
the member of the National Assembly expires;

#### Sub-clause (d)

(d)
In case he or she is absent   in ten consecutive meetings without
providing information to the concerned House;

#### Sub-clause (e)

(e)
In case the political party of which he or she was a member when elected
provides a notification in accordance with the federal law that he or she
has defected from the party,

#### Sub-clause (f)

(f)
In case he or she dies.
