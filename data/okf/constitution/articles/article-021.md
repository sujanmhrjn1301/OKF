# Article 21 -- Right of Victim of Crime

> **Part 3:** Fundamental Rights and Duties  
> **Page:** 12

### Clause (1)

(1) A victim of crime shall have the right to get information
about the investigation and proceedings of a case in which he or she is the victim.

### Clause (2)

(2) A victim of crime shall have the right to justice along with social
rehabilitation and compensation in accordance with law.
