# Article 116 -- Federal Consolidated Fund

> **Part 10:** Federal Financial Procedures  
> **Page:** 63

Except for the revenues of trusts (Guthi), all revenues
received by the Government of Nepal, all loans raised on the security of revenues, all
amounts received in repayment of any loan made under the authority of any Act and
any other moneys received by the Government of Nepal shall, except as otherwise
provided by a Federal Act, be credited to a Government Fund to be known as the
Federal Consolidated Fund.
