# Article 127 -- Courts

> **Part 11:** Judiciary  
> **Page:** 67

### Clause (1)

(1) There shall be the following courts in Nepal:-

#### Sub-clause (1)(a)

(a)
Supreme Court;

#### Sub-clause (1)(b)

(b)
High Court; and

#### Sub-clause (1)(c)

(c)
District Court.

### Clause (2)

(2) In addition to the courts referred to in clause (1), judicial institutions may
be constituted at the local level to try cases under law or other institutions as required
may be formed to pursue alternative dispute resolution methods.
