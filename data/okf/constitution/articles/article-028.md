# Article 28 -- Right to Privacy

> **Part 3:** Fundamental Rights and Duties  
> **Page:** 15

The privacy of any person, his or her residence, property, document,
data, correspondence and matters relating to his or her character shall, except in
accordance with law, be inviolable.
