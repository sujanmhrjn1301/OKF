# Article 237 -- Not to Affect Jurisdiction of Constitutional Bench of Supreme Court

> **Part 20:** Interrelations between Federation, Province and Local Level  
> **Page:** 121

Nothing
contained in this Part shall affect the jurisdiction of the Constitutional Bench of the
Supreme Court referred to in Article 137.
