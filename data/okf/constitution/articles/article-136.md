# Article 136 -- Responsibility of Chief Justice

> **Part 11:** Judiciary  
> **Page:** 71

The Chief Justice shall have the ultimate
responsibility to make effective the administration of justice by the Supreme Court,
subordinate courts, specialized courts or other judicial institution.
