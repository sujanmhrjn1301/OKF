# Article 286 -- Election Constituency Delimitation Commission

> **Part 32:** Miscellaneous  
> **Page:** 167

### Clause (1)

(1) The Government of Nepal may
constitute an Election Constituency Delimitation Commission, to determine election
constituencies for the purpose of election to the members of the Federal Parliament
and members of the Provincial Assemblies in accordance with this Constitution, which
shall consist of the following as the Chairperson and members:-

#### Sub-clause (1)(a)

(a) A retired Justice of Supreme Court
-Chairperson

#### Sub-clause (1)(b)

(b) One Geographer
-Member

#### Sub-clause (1)(c)

(c) One Sociologist or Demographer
-Member

#### Sub-clause (1)(d)

(d) One Administration Expert or Jurist
-Member

#### Sub-clause (1)(e)

(e) Gazetted
special
class
officer
of
the
Government of Nepal.
-Member-secretary

### Clause (2)

(2) The term of office of the Election Constituency Delimitation Commission
shall be as prescribed at the time of its constitution.

### Clause (3)

(3) A person shall be eligible to be appointed as the Chairperson or a member
of the Election Constituency Delimitation Commission in case he or she possesses the
following qualifications:-

#### Sub-clause (3)(a)

(a)
One who has acquired at least bachelor’s degree in related subject
from a recognized university;

#### Sub-clause (3)(b)

(b)
One who has attained the age of forty-five years; and

#### Sub-clause (3)(c)

(c)
One who bears high moral character.

### Clause (4)

(4) The office of the Chairperson or a member of the Election Constituency
Delimitation Commission shall fall vacant in any of the following circumstances: -

#### Sub-clause (4)(a)

(a)
In case he or she tenders resignation in writing;

#### Sub-clause (4)(b)

(b)
In case he or she is removed by the Government of Nepal, Council of
Ministers;

#### Sub-clause (4)(c)

(c)
In case he or she dies.
(5) While determining election constituencies pursuant to this Article,
the Election Constituency Delimitation Commission shall, subject to sub-clause

#### Sub-clause (4)(a)

(a) of clause (1) of Article 84, determine the constituencies in a Province in
accordance with the federal law, having regard to the population as the main
basis and geography as the second basis for representation, and there shall be at
least one election constituency in each district within the Province.

### Clause (6)

(6) While delimitating election constituencies pursuant to clause (5),
attention shall be required to be given, inter alia,
the population and
geographical convenience, density of population, geographical specificity,
administrative and transportation convenience, community and cultural aspects of the
constituencies.

### Clause (7)

(7) No question may be raised in any court on any matter of the determination,
or review, of election constituencies made by the Election Constituency Delimitation
Commission.

### Clause (8)

(8) The Election Constituency Delimitation Commission shall submit a report
on the works it has performed to the Government of Nepal.

### Clause (9)

(9) The Government of Nepal, Council of Ministers, shall lay the report
received pursuant to clause (8) before the Federal Parliament and shall also send it to
the Election Commission for implementation.

### Clause (10)

(10) The Election Constituency Delimitation Commission shall determine its
rules of procedures itself.

 Amended by the First Amendment.
 Inserted by the First Amendment.

### Clause (11)

(11) The remuneration and facilities of the Chairperson and the members of
the Election Constituency Delimitation Commission shall be similar to those of the
Chief Election Commissioner and the Election Commissioners of the Election
Commission, respectively.

### Clause (12)

(12) The election constituencies delimited pursuant to clause (5) shall be
reviewed in every twenty years.

### Clause (13)

(13) The Government of Nepal shall provide such employees as may be
required for the Election Constituency Delimitation Commission.
