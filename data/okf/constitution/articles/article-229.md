# Article 229 -- Local Consolidated Fund

> **Part 19:** Local Financial Procedures  
> **Page:** 118

### Clause (1)

(1) There shall be a Local Consolidate Fund in each Rural
Municipality and Municipality under the Local Level. All revenues received by the Rural
Municipality or Municipality, any amounts of grants received from the Government of
Nepal and the Provincial Government, all loans raised by the Rural Municipality and
Municipality, and amounts received from other sources shall be credited to such Fund.

### Clause (2)

(2) Matters relating to expenditures from the Local Consolidated Fund referred
to in clause (1) shall be as provided for in a Local law.
