# Article 67 -- Vice-President

> **Part 6:** President and Vice-President  
> **Page:** 40

### Clause (1)

(1) There shall be a Vice-president of Nepal.

### Clause (2)

(2) The functions to be performed by the President shall be performed by the
Vice-president during the absence of the President.

### Clause (3)

(3) In case a person who holds a political office to be fulfilled by way of
election, nomination or appointment is elected as the Vice-president, such office shall,
ipso facto, fall vacant.
