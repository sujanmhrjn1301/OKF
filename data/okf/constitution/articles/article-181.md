# Article 181 -- Decision as to Disqualification of Member

> **Part 14:** Provincial Legislature  
> **Page:** 95

In case question arises as to whether any
member of the Provincial Assembly is disqualified or has ceased to possess any of the
qualifications referred to in Article 178, the Constitutional Bench of the Supreme
Court shall make the final decision of such question.
