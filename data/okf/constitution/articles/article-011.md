# Article 11 -- To be Citizens of Nepal

> **Part 2:** Citizenship  
> **Page:** 5

### Clause (1)

(1) The persons who have obtained the citizenship of Nepal
at the time of commencement of this Constitution and who are qualified to obtain
citizenship in accordance with this Part shall be the citizens of Nepal.

### Clause (2)

(2) The following person who has his or her permanent domicile in Nepal at
the time of commencement of this Constitution shall be the citizen of Nepal by
descent:-

#### Sub-clause (2)(a)

(a)
A person who has obtained the citizenship of Nepal by descent prior
to the commencement of this Constitution;

#### Sub-clause (2)(b)

(b)
A person whose father or mother was a citizen of Nepal at the time of
his or her birth.

### Clause (3)

(3) A child of a citizen having obtained the citizenship of Nepal by virtue of
birth prior to the commencement of this Constitution shall, upon attaining the age of
majority, acquire the citizenship of Nepal by descent in case the child's father and
mother both are citizens of Nepal.

### Clause (4)

(4) Every minor who is found within Nepal and the whereabouts of whose
father and mother are not known shall, until the father or the mother of the child is
traced, be a citizen of Nepal by descent.

### Clause (5)

(5) A person who is born in Nepal to a woman who is a citizen of Nepal and
has resided in Nepal and whose father is not traced shall be provided with the
citizenship of Nepal by descent.

#### Proviso-1

> Provided that in case his or her father is held to be a foreign citizen, the
citizenship of such person shall be converted into naturalized citizenship as provided
for in the federal law.

### Clause (6)

(6) A foreign woman who has a matrimonial relationship with a citizen of
Nepal may, in case she so desires, acquire the naturalized citizenship of Nepal as
provided for in the federal law.

### Clause (7)

(7) Notwithstanding anything contained elsewhere in this Article, in the case
of a person born to a woman who is a citizen of Nepal and married to a foreign
citizen, the person may acquire the naturalized citizenship of Nepal in accordance
with the federal law in case he or she has permanently resided in Nepal and has not
acquired the citizenship of a foreign country.

#### Proviso-2

> Provided that in case such person's mother and father both are citizens of
Nepal at the time of acquisition of citizenship, such person born in Nepal may acquire
the citizenship of Nepal by descent.

### Clause (8)

(8) In the cases other than those referred to in this Article, the Government of
Nepal may, in accordance with the federal law, grant the naturalized citizenship of
Nepal.

### Clause (9)

(9) The Government of Nepal may, in accordance with the federal law, grant
the honorary citizenship of Nepal.

### Clause (10)

(10) Whenever any territory is acquired by way of incorporation into Nepal, a
person having his or her domicile within such territory shall, subject to the federal
law, become a citizen of Nepal.
