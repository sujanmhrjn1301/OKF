# Article 193 -- Power of Provincial Assembly to Constitute Committees

> **Part 14:** Provincial Legislature  
> **Page:** 100

The Provincial Assembly
may, in accordance with its rules, constitute committees or special committees, as
required, in order to manage its working procedures.
