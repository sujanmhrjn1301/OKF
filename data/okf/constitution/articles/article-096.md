# Article 96 -- Deputy Prime Minister, Minister, Minister of State and Assistant Minister entitled to take part in both Houses

> **Part 8:** Federal Legislature  
> **Page:** 53

#### Proviso-1

> Provided that he or she shall not be entitled to vote in a House or its committee
of which he or she is not a member.
