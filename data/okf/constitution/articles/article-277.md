# Article 277 -- Titles, Honours and Decorations

> **Part 32:** Miscellaneous  
> **Page:** 164

### Clause (1)

(1) The President shall confer titles, honours and
decorations to be conferred on behalf of the State.

### Clause (2)

(2) No citizen of Nepal may, without the approval of the Government of
Nepal, accept any title, honour or decoration from any foreign government.
 278. Power to Conclude Treaty: (1) The Federation shall have the power to conclude
treaties or agreements.

### Clause (2)

(2) In concluding a treaty or agreement on a matter falling within the list of
powers of Province, the Government of Nepal shall be required to consult the
concerned Province.

### Clause (3)

(3) A Provincial Council of Ministers may, with the consent of the
Government of Nepal, enter into contractual agreements on financial and industrial
matters.
