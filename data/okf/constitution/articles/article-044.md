# Article 44 -- Rights of Consumer

> **Part 3:** Fundamental Rights and Duties  
> **Page:** 20

### Clause (1)

(1) Every consumer shall have the right to obtain quality goods
and services.

### Clause (2)

(2) A person who has suffered damage from any substandard goods or services
shall have the right to obtain compensation in accordance with law.
