# Article 33 -- Right to Employment

> **Part 3:** Fundamental Rights and Duties  
> **Page:** 16

### Clause (1)

(1) Every citizen shall have the right to employment. The terms
and conditions of employment, and unemployment benefit shall be as provided for in
the federal law.

### Clause (2)

(2) Every citizen shall have the right to choose employment.
