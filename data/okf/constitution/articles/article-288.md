# Article 288 -- Capital

> **Part 32:** Miscellaneous  
> **Page:** 170

### Clause (1)

(1) The capital of Nepal shall be located in Kathmandu.

### Clause (2)

(2) The capital of a Province in accordance with this Constitution shall be as
decided by a two-thirds majority of the number of the then members of the concerned
Provincial Assembly.

### Clause (3)

(3) The business of a Province shall be conducted through the place as
specified by the Government of Nepal until a decision is made pursuant to clause (2).
