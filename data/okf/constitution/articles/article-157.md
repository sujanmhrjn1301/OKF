# Article 157 -- Attorney General

> **Part 12:** Attorney General  
> **Page:** 82

### Clause (1)

(1) There shall be an Attorney General of Nepal.

### Clause (2)

(2) The President shall, on recommendation of the Prime Minister, appoint the
Attorney General.  The Attorney General shall hold his or her office during the
pleasure of the Prime Minister.

### Clause (3)

(3) A person who is eligible   to be appointed as a Justice of the Supreme
Court shall be eligible to be appointed as the Attorney General.

### Clause (4)

(4) The office of the Attorney General shall fall vacant in any of the following
circumstances:

#### Sub-clause (4)(a)

(a)
In case he or she tenders resignation in writing to the President
through the Prime Minister;

#### Sub-clause (4)(b)

(b)
In case he or she is relieved of the office by the President on
recommendation of the Prime Minister;

#### Sub-clause (4)(c)

(c)
In case he or she dies.

### Clause (5)

(5) The remuneration and other facilities of the Attorney General shall be
similar to those of a Justice of Supreme Court. Other conditions of service of the
Attorney General shall be in accordance with law.
