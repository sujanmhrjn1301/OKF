# Article 68 -- Vacation of Office of Vice-President

> **Part 6:** President and Vice-President  
> **Page:** 40

#### Sub-clause (a)

(a)
In case he or she tenders resignation in writing to the President,

#### Sub-clause (b)

(b)
In case a motion of impeachment against him or her is adopted under
Article 101,

#### Sub-clause (c)

(c)
In case his or her term of office expires,

#### Sub-clause (d)

(d)
In case he or she dies.
