# Article 238 -- Commission for the Investigation of Abuse of Authority

> **Part 21:** Commission for the Investigation of Abuse of Authority  
> **Page:** 122

### Clause (1)

(1) There shall be a
Commission for the Investigation of Abuse of Authority of Nepal consisting of the
Chief Commissioner and four other Commissioners. The Chief Commissioner shall
act as the Chairperson of the Commission for the Investigation of Abuse of Authority.

### Clause (2)

(2) The President shall, on recommendation of the Constitutional Council,
appoint the Chief Commissioner and Commissioners.

### Clause (3)

(3) The term of office of the Chief Commissioner and Commissioners shall be
six years from the date of appointment.

### Clause (4)

(4) Notwithstanding anything contained in clause (3), the office of the Chief
Commissioner or a Commissioner shall fall vacant in any of the following
circumstances: -

#### Sub-clause (4)(a)

(a)
In case he or she tenders resignation in writing to the President;

#### Sub-clause (4)(b)

(b)
In case he or she attains the age of sixty-five years,

#### Sub-clause (4)(c)

(c)
In case a motion of impeachment is adopted against him or her
pursuant to Article 101;

#### Sub-clause (4)(d)

(d)
In case he or she is removed from office by the President on
recommendation of the Constitutional Council on grounds of his or
her inability to hold office and discharge the functions due to
physical or mental illness;

#### Sub-clause (4)(e)

(e)
In case he or she dies.

### Clause (5)

(5) The Chief Commissioner and Commissioners appointed pursuant to clause

### Clause (2)

(2) shall not be eligible for reappointment.

#### Proviso-1

> Provided that a Commissioner may be appointed to the office of Chief
Commissioner, and when a Commissioner is so appointed as the Chief Commissioner,
his or her term of office shall be so computed as to include his or her term as the
Commissioner.

### Clause (6)

(6) A person shall be eligible to be appointed as the Chief Commissioner or a
Commissioner of the Commission for the Investigation of Abuse of Authority in case
he or she possesses the following qualifications:-

#### Sub-clause (6)(a)

(a)
One who has acquired a bachelor's degree from a recognized
university;

#### Sub-clause (6)(b)

(b)
One who is not a member of any political party at the time of
appointment;

#### Sub-clause (6)(c)

(c)
One who has gained at least twenty years of experiences in the field
of either accounting, revenue, engineering, law, development or
research and being a distinguished person;

#### Sub-clause (6)(d)

(d)
One who has attained the age of forty-five years; and

#### Sub-clause (6)(e)

(e)
One who bears high moral character.

### Clause (7)

(7) The remuneration and other terms and conditions of service of the Chief
Commissioner and the Commissioners shall be as provided for in the federal law. The
remuneration and terms and conditions of service of the Chief Commissioner and the
Commissioners shall not, so long as they hold office, be altered to their disadvantage.

#### Proviso-2

> Provided that this provision shall not apply in cases where a state of
emergency is declared because of extreme economic disarrays.

### Clause (8)

(8) A person once appointed as the Chief Commissioner or Commissioner
shall not be eligible for appointment in other government service.

#### Proviso-3

> Provided that nothing in this clause shall be deemed to be a bar to the
appointment to any political position or to any position which has the responsibility of
making investigations, inquiries or findings on any subject, or to any position which
has the responsibility of submitting advice, opinion or recommendation after carrying
out a study or research on any subject.
