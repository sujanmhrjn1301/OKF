# Article 131 -- Vacation of office of Chief Justice or Justice of Supreme Court

> **Part 11:** Judiciary  
> **Page:** 69

#### Sub-clause (a)

(a)
In case he or she tenders resignation in writing to the President;

#### Sub-clause (b)

(b)
In case he or she attains the age of sixty-five years;

#### Sub-clause (c)

(c)
In case a motion of impeachment is adopted against him or her pursuant
to Article 101;

#### Sub-clause (d)

(d)  In case the Chief Justice or a Justice of Supreme Court is relieved of his
or her office by the President, in the case of Chief Justice on
recommendation of the Constitutional Council, and in the case of Justice
of Supreme Court, on recommendation of the Judicial Council, on the
ground that he or she is unable to discharge his or her duties because of
physical or mental illness;

#### Sub-clause (e)

(e)
In case he or she is punished by a court for a criminal offence involving
moral turpitude;

#### Sub-clause (f)

(f)
In case he or she dies.
