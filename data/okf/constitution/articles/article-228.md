# Article 228 -- No Tax to be levied or Loan to be raised

> **Part 19:** Local Financial Procedures  
> **Page:** 118

### Clause (1)

(1) No tax shall be levied and collected and
no loan raised in the Local Level except in accordance with law.

### Clause (2)

(2) The Local Level may levy tax by making a law on matters falling within its
jurisdiction without prejudice to national economic policies, carriage of goods and
services, capital and labour market, and the neighbouring Province or Local Level.
