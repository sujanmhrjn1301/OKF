# Article 81 -- To inform President

> **Part 7:** Federal Executive  
> **Page:** 45

#### Sub-clause (a)

(a)
Resolutions of the Council of Ministers;

#### Sub-clause (b)

(b)
Bills to be introduced in the Federal Parliament;

#### Sub-clause (c)

(c)
Such other necessary information as commanded by the President on
matters set forth in clauses (a) and (b); and

#### Sub-clause (d)

(d)
Current general state of affairs of Nepal and matters concerning foreign
relations.
