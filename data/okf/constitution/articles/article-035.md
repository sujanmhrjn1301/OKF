# Article 35 -- Right relating to Health

> **Part 3:** Fundamental Rights and Duties  
> **Page:** 17

### Clause (1)

(1) Every citizen shall have the right to free basic health
services from the State, and no one shall be deprived of emergency health services.

### Clause (2)

(2) Every person shall have the right to get information about his or her medical
treatment.

### Clause (3)

(3) Every citizen shall have equal access to health services.

### Clause (4)

(4) Every citizen shall have the right of access to clean drinking water and
sanitation.
