# Article 125 -- Act relating to Financial Procedures

> **Part 10:** Federal Financial Procedures  
> **Page:** 66

Matters relating to the transfer of moneys
appropriated by a Federal Act from one head to another and other financial procedures
shall be as provided for in the Federal Act.
