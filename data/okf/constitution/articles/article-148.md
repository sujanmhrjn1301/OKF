# Article 148 -- District Court

> **Part 11:** Judiciary  
> **Page:** 76

### Clause (1)

(1) There shall be a District Court in each District.

### Clause (2)

(2) The Local Level judicial institutions established in accordance with the
provincial law shall be subordinate to the District Court. The District Court may
inspect as well as supervise and give necessary direction to its subordinate judicial
institutions.
