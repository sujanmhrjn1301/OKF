# Article 143 -- Chief Judge and Judges not to be engaged in any Other Office and Provisions relating to Transfer

> **Part 11:** Judiciary  
> **Page:** 75

### Clause (1)

(1) No Chief Judge or Judge of a High Court shall be engaged in or
deputed to any office other than that of Judge.

#### Proviso-1

> Provided that the Government of Nepal may, in consultation with the Judicial
Council, depute a Judge of the High Court to work concerning judicial inquiry, or to
legal or judicial investigation or research or to any work of national interest for any
specific period.

### Clause (2)

(2) The Chief Justice may, on recommendation of the Judicial Council,
transfer a Judge of a High Court to another High Court.
