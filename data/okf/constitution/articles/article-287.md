# Article 287 -- Language Commission

> **Part 32:** Miscellaneous  
> **Page:** 169

### Clause (1)

(1) The Government of Nepal shall, within one year of the
commencement of this Constitution, constitute a Language Commission comprising
representation of Provinces.

### Clause (2)

(2) The Language Commission shall consist of one Chairperson and a required
number of members.

### Clause (3)

(3) The term of office of the Chairperson and members of the Language
Commission shall be six years from the date of their appointment. They may not be
reappointed.

### Clause (4)

(4) A person shall be eligible to be appointed as the Chairperson or a member
of the Language Commission in case he or she possesses the following
qualifications:-

#### Sub-clause (4)(a)

(a)
One who has acquired Master’s degree in related subject from a
recognized university;

#### Sub-clause (4)(b)

(b)
One who has gained at least twenty years of experience in the fields
of study, teaching and research on various languages of Nepal;

#### Sub-clause (4)(c)

(c)
One who has completed the age of forty-five years; and

#### Sub-clause (4)(d)

(d)
One who bears high moral character.

### Clause (5)

(5) The office of the Chairperson or a member of the Language Commission
shall fall vacant in any of the following circumstances:-

#### Sub-clause (5)(a)

(a)
In case he or she tenders resignation in writing;

#### Sub-clause (5)(b)

(b)
In case he or she is removed by the Government of Nepal, Council
of Ministers;

#### Sub-clause (5)(c)

(c)
In case he completes the age of sixty-five years;

#### Sub-clause (5)(d)

(d)
In case he or she dies.

### Clause (6)

(6)
The functions, duties and powers of the Language Commission shall
be as follows:-

#### Sub-clause (6)(a)

(a)
To determine the criteria to be fulfilled for the recognition as the
official language and make recommendations on languages to the
Government of Nepal;

#### Sub-clause (6)(b)

(b)
To make recommendations to the Government of Nepal, on the
measures to be adopted for the protection, promotion and
development of languages;

#### Sub-clause (6)(c)

(c)
To measure the levels of development of mother tongues and make
suggestions to the Government of Nepal, on the potentiality of their
use in education;

#### Sub-clause (6)(d)

(d)
To conduct study and research on languages and monitor them.

### Clause (7)

(7) The Language Commission shall complete its tasks referred to in sub-
clause (a) of clause (6) within five years after the date of its constitution.

### Clause (8)

(8) The Government of Nepal may, in coordination with the Provincial
Government, establish a branch of the Language Commission in the Province.

### Clause (9)

(9) The other functions, duties, powers and rules of procedures of the
Language Commission shall be as provided for in federal law.
