# Article 307 -- Short Title and Commencement

> **Part 35:** Short Title, Commencement and Repeal  
> **Page:** 184

### Clause (1)

(1) This Constitution may be cited as the
“Constitution of Nepal".

### Clause (2)

(2) This Constitution shall commence on Twenty September, Two Thousand
Fifteen A.D. (corresponding to the third day of the month of Ashwin of the year Two
Thousand Seventy Two of Bikram Era).
