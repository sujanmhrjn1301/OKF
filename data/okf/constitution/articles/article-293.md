# Article 293 -- Monitoring of Functioning of Constitutional Bodies

> **Part 32:** Miscellaneous  
> **Page:** 172

The chiefs and officials of the
Constitutional Bodies shall be accountable and responsible to the Federal Parliament.
The committees of the House of Representatives may monitor and evaluate the
functioning, including reports, of the Constitutional Bodies, other than the National
Human Rights Commission, and give necessary direction or advice.
