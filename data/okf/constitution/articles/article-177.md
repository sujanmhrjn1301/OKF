# Article 177 -- Term of Provincial Assembly

> **Part 14:** Provincial Legislature  
> **Page:** 93

### Clause (1)

(1) Unless dissolved earlier in accordance with this
Constitution, the term of the Provincial Assembly shall be five years.

### Clause (2)

(2) Notwithstanding anything contained in clause (1), the term of the
Provincial Assembly may be extended by a provincial Act for a period not exceeding
one year in cases where a proclamation or order of a state of emergency is in effect.

### Clause (3)

(3) The term of the Provincial Assembly extended pursuant to clause (2) shall,
ipso facto, be terminated after the expiry of six months from the date of revocation of
the proclamation or order of the state of emergency in the concerned Province.
