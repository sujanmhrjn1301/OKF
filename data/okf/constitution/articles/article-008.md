# Article 8 -- National Flag

> **Part 1:** Preliminary  
> **Page:** 4

### Clause (1)

(1) The national flag of Nepal consists of two juxtaposed triangular
figures with a crimson-coloured base and deep blue borders, there being a white
emblem of the crescent moon with eight rays visible out of sixteen in the upper part
and a white emblem of a twelve rayed sun in the lower part.

### Clause (2)

(2) The method of drawing out the flag and other particulars relating thereto
shall be as provided for in Schedule-1.
