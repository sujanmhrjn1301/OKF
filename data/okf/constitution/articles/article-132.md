# Article 132 -- Chief Justice and Justice of Supreme Court not to be engaged in Any Other Office

> **Part 11:** Judiciary  
> **Page:** 69

### Clause (1)

(1)
No Chief Justice or Justice of the Supreme Court shall be engaged in or deputed to
any office other than that of a Judge.

#### Proviso-1

> Provided that the Government of Nepal may, in consultation with the Judicial
Council, depute a Justice of the Supreme Court to work concerning judicial inquiry, or
to legal or judicial investigation or research for a specified period.

### Clause (2)

(2) No person who has once held the office of Chief Justice or a Justice of the
Supreme Court shall be eligible for appointment to any government office, except as
otherwise provided for in this Constitution.
