# Article 234 -- Inter-Province Council

> **Part 20:** Interrelations between Federation, Province and Local Level  
> **Page:** 120

### Clause (1)

(1) There shall be an Inter-Province Council as follows to
settle political disputes arising out between the Federation and a Province and
between Provinces:-

#### Sub-clause (1)(a)

(a)
Prime Minister
              - Chairperson

#### Sub-clause (1)(b)

(b)
Minister for Home Affairs of the Government of Nepal
 - Member

#### Sub-clause (1)(c)

(c)
Minister for Finance of the Government of Nepal
   - Member

#### Sub-clause (1)(d)

(d)
Chief Ministers of the concerned Province
              - Member

### Clause (2)

(2) The Inter-Province Council may meet as required.

### Clause (3)

(3) The Inter-Province Council may invite to its meeting a Minister of the
Government of Nepal and a Minister of the concerned Province who is responsible for
the matter of dispute and a concerned expert.

### Clause (4)

(4) The rules of procedures of the meeting of the Inter-Province Council shall
be as determined by the Council itself.
