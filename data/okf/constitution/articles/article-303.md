# Article 303 -- Provisions relating to Local Bodies

> **Part 33:** Transitional Provisions  
> **Page:** 180

### Clause (1)

(1) The Local Bodies existing at the time of
commencement of this Constitution shall continue to exist until the determination of
the number and areas of the Local Level in accordance with this Constitution.

### Clause (2)

(2) Election to the officials of the Local Bodies existing pursuant to clause (1)
shall be held in accordance with law.

### Clause (3)

(3) The officials of the Local Bodies elected in pursuant to clause (2) shall
continue to hold office until election to the Local Level is held in accordance with this
Constitution.
