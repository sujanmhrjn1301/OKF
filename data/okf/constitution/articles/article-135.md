# Article 135 -- Not to be engaged in Practice of Law

> **Part 11:** Judiciary  
> **Page:** 71

No Chief Justice or Justice of the Supreme
Court shall, after retirement from office, be engaged in the practice of law, mediation
or arbitration proceedings before any office or court.
