# Article 306 -- Definitions and Interpretations

> **Part 34:** Definitions and Interpretations  
> **Page:** 182

### Clause (1)

(1) Unless the subject or the context otherwise
requires, in this Constitution,-

#### Sub-clause (1)(a)

(a)
"Minority" means ethnic, linguistic and religious groups whose
population is less than the percentage specified by the federal law, and
includes groups that have their distinct ethnic, religious or linguistic
characteristics, aspirations to protect such characteristics and subjected
to discrimination and oppression.

#### Sub-clause (1)(b)

(b)
"Law" means federal law, provincial law and local law.

#### Sub-clause (1)(c)

(c)
"Article" means an Article of this Constitution.

#### Sub-clause (1)(d)

(d)
"Municipality" means and includes a Metropolitan City and Sub-
Metropolitan City.

#### Sub-clause (1)(e)

(e)
"Citizen" means a citizen of Nepal.

#### Sub-clause (1)(f)

(f)
"Province" means the area and form of a federal unit of Nepal divided
into the federal units in accordance with this Constitution.

#### Sub-clause (1)(g)

(g)
"Remuneration" means and includes salary, allowance, pension and
any other form of emolument and facility.

#### Sub-clause (1)(h)

(h)
"State power" means the power relating to the Executive, Legislative
and Judiciary of the State, and includes residuary power as well.

#### Sub-clause (1)(i)

(i)
"Bill" means a draft of amendment to the Constitution or of an Act,
which has been introduced in the Federal Parliament or a Provincial
Assembly, as the case may be.

#### Sub-clause (1)(j)

(j)
"Federation" means the federal level that is the apex unit of the federal
structure.

#### Sub-clause (1)(k)

(k)
"Federal Units" means the Federation, Province and Local Level.

#### Sub-clause (1)(l)

(l)
"Constitutional Bodies" means the Commission for the Investigation of
Abuse of Authority, Auditor General, Public Service Commission,

Election Commission, National Human Rights Commission, National
Natural Resources and Fiscal Commission, National Women
Commission,
National
Dalit
Commission,
National
Inclusion
Commission,
Indigenous
Nationalities
Commission,
Madhesi
Commission, Tharu Commission and Muslim Commission.

#### Sub-clause (1)(m)

(m)
"Marginalized" means communities that are made politically,
economically and socially backward, are unable to enjoy services and
facilities because of discrimination and oppression and of geographical
remoteness or deprived thereof and are in lower status than the human
development standards under federal law, and includes highly
marginalized groups and groups on the verge of extinction.

#### Sub-clause (1)(n)

(n)
"Local Level" means the Rural Municipality, Municipality and District
Assembly to be established in accordance with this Constitution.

### Clause (2)

(2) Unless the subject or the context otherwise requires, the legal provisions
relating to interpretation of laws shall, subject to the provisions of this Constitution,
apply to the interpretation of this Constitution in the same manner as that provisions
apply to the interpretation of the Nepal laws.
