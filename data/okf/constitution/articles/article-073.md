# Article 73 -- Office of President and Vice-President

> **Part 6:** President and Vice-President  
> **Page:** 40

### Clause (1)

(1) There shall be separate offices for the
performance of the functions of the President and the Vice-President.

### Clause (2)

(2) The Government of Nepal shall make arrangements for employees and
other provisions as required to perform the functions of the offices referred to in
clause (1).
