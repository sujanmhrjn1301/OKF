# Article 240 -- Auditor General

> **Part 22:** Auditor General  
> **Page:** 125

### Clause (1)

(1) There shall be an Auditor General of Nepal.

### Clause (2)

(2) The President shall, on the recommendation of the Constitutional Council,
appoint the Auditor General.

### Clause (3)

(3) The term of office of the Auditor General shall be six years from the date
of appointment.

### Clause (4)

(4) Notwithstanding anything contained in clause (3), the office of the Auditor
General shall fall vacant in any of the following circumstances: -

#### Sub-clause (4)(a)

(a)
In case he or she tenders resignation in writing to the President;

#### Sub-clause (4)(b)

(b)
In case he or she attains the age of sixty-five years;

#### Sub-clause (4)(c)

(c)
In case a motion of impeachment is adopted against him or her
pursuant to Article 101;

#### Sub-clause (4)(d)

(d)
In case he or she is removed from office by the President on
recommendation of the Constitutional Council on grounds of his or
her inability to hold office and discharge the functions due to physical
or mental illness;

#### Sub-clause (4)(e)

(e)
In case he or she dies.

### Clause (5)

(5) The Auditor General appointed pursuant to clause (2) shall not be eligible
for reappointment.

### Clause (6)

(6) A person shall be eligible to be appointed as the Auditor General in case he
or she possesses the following qualifications:-

#### Sub-clause (6)(a)

(a)
One who has served in the Special Class of the Government of Nepal
or having at least twenty years of experiences in audit related work,
after having obtained a bachelor's degree in management, commerce
or accounting from a recognized university or having passed a
chartered accountancy examination;

#### Sub-clause (6)(b)

(b)
One who is not a member of any political party at the time of
appointment;

#### Sub-clause (6)(c)

(c)
One who has attained the age of forty-five years; and

#### Sub-clause (6)(d)

(d)
One who bears high moral character.

### Clause (7)

(7) The remuneration and terms and conditions of service of the Auditor
General shall be as provided for in the federal law. The remuneration and conditions
of service of the Auditor General shall not, so long as he or she holds office, be
altered to his or her disadvantage.

#### Proviso-1

> Provided that this provision shall not apply in cases where a state of
emergency is declared because of extreme economic disarrays.

### Clause (8)

(8) A person once appointed as the Auditor General shall not be eligible for
appointment in another government service.

#### Proviso-2

> Provided that nothing in this clause shall be deemed to be a bar to the
appointment to any political position or to any position which has the responsibility of
making investigations, inquiries or findings on any subject, or to any position which
has the responsibility of submitting advice, opinion or recommendation after carrying
out a study or research on any subject.
