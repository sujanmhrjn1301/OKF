# Article 199 -- Procedures for Adoption of Bills

> **Part 15:** Provincial Legislative Procedures  
> **Page:** 103

### Clause (1)

(1) A Bill adopted by the Provincial Assembly shall
be submitted to the Chief of Province for authentication.

### Clause (2)

(2) In case a session of the Provincial Assembly is prorogued while a Bill is
under consideration, deliberations on the Bill may continue at the succeeding session.

#### Proviso-1

> Provided that in case the Provincial Assembly is dissolved or its term expires
when any Bill is under its consideration, such Bill shall lapse.
