# Section 94: Not to arrest attorney

**Act:** The National Criminal Procedure (Code) Act, 2017  
**Chapter 10:** Provisions Relating to Transfer and Adjournment of Cases  
*Page 105*

---

No attorney shall be arrested for any 
sentence imposed on, or liability to be borne by, the party upon 
passing judgment in the case.
