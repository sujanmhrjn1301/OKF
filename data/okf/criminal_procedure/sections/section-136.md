# Section 136: Procedures to be fulfilled in making appeal

**Act:** The National Criminal Procedure (Code) Act, 2017  
**Chapter 14:** Provisions Relating to Appeal and Reference for Sanction  
*Page 145*

---

### Sub-section (1)
A memorandum 
of appeal shall be made in the form referred to in Schedule-45.

### Sub-section (2)
Every memorandum of appeal to be made pursuant to 
sub-section (1) shall clearly state, inter alia, the full name and 
address of the appellant and that of the respondent, short description 
of the case, the matters contained in the decision of the lower court 
with which the appellant is dissatisfied and grounds for such 
dissatisfaction, the matters to be decided by the appeal hearing court 
and the relevant laws, and shall bear signature or thumb impression 
of the appellant.

### Sub-section (3)
Every memorandum of appeal to be made pursuant to 
sub-section (1) shall be accompanied by the following documents:

- **Clause (a)**: A copy of the judgment intended to be appealed 
against,
- **Clause (b)**: If any sentence is imposed on the appellant by the 
judgment, evidence showing that he or she has 
served or paid such sentence or has been 
imprisoned or furnished a bail/bond or guarantee 
for the same.
