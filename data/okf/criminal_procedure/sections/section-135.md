# Section 135: Filing of appeal through judgment making court or prison where 
one is imprisoned

**Act:** The National Criminal Procedure (Code) Act, 2017  
**Chapter 14:** Provisions Relating to Appeal and Reference for Sanction  
*Page 144*

---

### Sub-section (1)
Notwithstanding anything contained in sub-
section (2) of Section 134, any person wishing to make appeal 

 
 
pursuant to sub-section (1) of that Section may make appeal through 
the judgment making court or also through the prison where the 
person convicted of the offence pursuant to such judgment is 
imprisoned.

### Sub-section (2)
If an appeal is filed pursuant to sub-section (1), such 
court or prison shall, as soon as possible, forward the appeal to the 
appeal hearing court, setting out the details of the service or 
payment, if any, by the appellant of the sentence imposed on him or 
her or the bail/bond or guarantee, if any, furnished by him or her in 
consideration for such sentence, and give information thereof to the 
appellant.

### Sub-section (3)
The judgment making court shall forward the case-file 
along with the appeal pursuant to sub-section (2).
