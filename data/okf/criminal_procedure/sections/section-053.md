# Section 53: Complaint may be made

**Act:** The National Criminal Procedure (Code) Act, 2017  
**Chapter 5:** Provisions Relating to Preliminary Proceedings of Cases  
*Page 64*

---

### Sub-section (1)
Except as otherwise provided in this 
Act, the following person may file a complaint in relation to any 
offence in the court:

- **Clause (a)**: A person who has suffered injury from the offence,
- **Clause (b)**: In the case of any matter requiring compliant by a 
public servant who is deputed elsewhere or abroad in 
the course of his or her employment, any of his or her 
immediate successors,
- **Clause (c)**: In relation to any act or thing required to be done or 
preserved by any person by virtue of any post, office or 
any other capacity, the person holding such post, office 
or capacity,
- **Clause (d)**: In relation to any matter in which a person with speech 
or visual impairment, child or person over seventy-five 
years of age or person who is physically or mentally 
incompetent is entitled to make complaint, his or her 
successor living in the joint family and, failing such 
successor, any other successor or guardian,
- **Clause (e)**: In relation to any offence involving any body corporate, 
the director, member, administrator or chief executive 
officer who is authorized to act as the chief of such 
body.

### Sub-section (2)
A complaint referred to in sub-section (1) shall state the 
following matters and be in the form referred to in Schedule-21:

- **Clause (a)**: The name, surname, address (including the street), 
description, age, occupation or business of the accused, 
the name of his or her father, mother, husband or wife,
- **Clause (b)**: The citizenship, passport, driving license of the accused 
or his or her other descriptions that could enable to find 
him or her easily,
- **Clause (c)**: The Village Body or Municipality where the accused is 
residing or staying, ward number, village, street, block 
number, if available, and other descriptions of the 
accused to the extent of availability,
- **Clause (d)**: If the accused is staying in a rented house, the name, 
surname and address of the house owner, to the extent 
of availability,
- **Clause (e)**: If the accused has no permanent abode, the name, 
surname and address of the owner of house where the 
accused is staying,
- **Clause (f)**: The particulars related to the offence,
- **Clause (g)**: Where the offender has committed more than one 
offence at the same time, each count of charge made 
against him or her, and evidence related thereto,
- **Clause (h)**: The relevant law to be applied, and the reason for the 
same,
- **Clause (i)**: The sentence demanded to be imposed on the accused, 
and the ground for the same,
- **Clause (j)**: The 
injury 
caused 
from 
the 
offence, 
and 
if 
compensation has to be recovered from the accused, the 
amount of such compensation and the ground for the 
same,
- **Clause (k)**: Other necessary matters.

### Sub-section (3)
Any complaint referred to in sub-section (1) shall be 
accompanied by such number of copies of the complaint as that of 
the accused and one copy each of the documentary evidences 
intended to be produced by the complainant.

> **Provided that it is not required to submit copies of the 
complaint and documentary evidences in a case in which the 
Government of Nepal is plaintiff.**
