# Section 49: Cases under jurisdiction of both higher and lower courts to be 
tried by higher court

**Act:** The National Criminal Procedure (Code) Act, 2017  
**Chapter 5:** Provisions Relating to Preliminary Proceedings of Cases  
*Page 60*

---

If a case related to an offence falls under the 
jurisdiction of both the higher and the lower courts pursuant to the 
law in force or if a case related to a person or offence which is liable 
to joint proceeding under Section 54, being under the jurisdiction of 
both the higher and the lower courts, can be so filed or has been filed 
in such courts, the higher court shall have power to try such case.
