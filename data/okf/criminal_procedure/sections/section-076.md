# Section 76: Amount of bail/bond may be returned and guarantee released

**Act:** The National Criminal Procedure (Code) Act, 2017  
**Chapter 7:** Provisions Relating to Detention, Bail and Guarantee  
*Page 94*

---

### Sub-section (1)
If the court remands the accused in detention pursuant to this 
Chapter in a case filed in the court, the bail/bond or guarantee 
furnished by him or her before detention shall be released.

### Sub-section (2)
Except as otherwise provided in this Act, the bail/bond 
or guarantee taken by the court from any accused in the course of the 
proceedings of the case shall be immediately returned or released to 
the accused if the accused is ultimately adjudged to be acquitted of 
the offence.
