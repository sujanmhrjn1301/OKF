# Section 1: Short title and commencement

**Act:** The National Criminal Procedure (Code) Act, 2017  
**Chapter 1:** Preliminary  
*Page 1*

---

### Sub-section (1)
This Act may be cited as the 
"National Criminal Procedure (Code) Act, 2017".

### Sub-section (2)
Section 192 of this Act shall commence on such date as 
appointed by the Government of Nepal by a notification in the Nepal 
Gazette, and the other Sections, on 17 August 2018.
