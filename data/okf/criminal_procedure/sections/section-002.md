# Section 2: Definitions

**Act:** The National Criminal Procedure (Code) Act, 2017  
**Chapter 1:** Preliminary  
*Page 1*

---

### Sub-section (1)
A case related to a legal right, status, post, family 
relationship or property,

### Sub-section (2)
Establishment of relationship, or divorce,

### Sub-section (3)
Fee, remuneration, salary, allowance or wage,

### Sub-section (4)
Partition, 
succession, 
donation, 
gift, 
will, 
guardianship, curatorship, parental authority, 
adoption,

### Sub-section (5)
Any right or claim under any contract, quasi-
contract or unlawful enrichment,

### Sub-section (6)
Any right or claim relating to tort, quasi-tort or 
defective product,

### Sub-section (7)
Servitude,

### Sub-section (8)
Compensation, or

### Sub-section (9)
Any other right or liability of civil nature. 
(m) 
"case" means a criminal case; 

 
 
(n) 
"adjudicating authority" means an authority empowered by 
law to proceed, hear and adjudicate a case, and this term also 
includes, in the case of a person taken in custody, a court 
nearby the place where the person is taken in custody; 
(o) 
"Local Level" means a Village Body (Gownpalika) or 
Municipality; 
(p) "government attorney" means the Attorney General, Deputy 
Attorney General, Joint Attorney, Deputy Attorney, District 
Attorney or Assistant District Attorney, and this term also 
includes an officer designated by the Attorney General to act 
in the capacity of government attorney.
