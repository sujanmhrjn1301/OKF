# Section 179: To give notice on release of property subject to attachment

**Act:** The National Criminal Procedure (Code) Act, 2017  
**Chapter 16:** Miscellaneous  
*Page 186*

---

In 
releasing any property attached in accordance with this Act pursuant 
to the court judgment or order, the court shall give a notice thereof to 
the concerned office and the person responsible for such attachment.
