# Section 91: Party may be attorney

**Act:** The National Criminal Procedure (Code) Act, 2017  
**Chapter 10:** Provisions Relating to Transfer and Adjournment of Cases  
*Page 104*

---

In any case involving more than one 
plaintiff or defendant, any one of them may be appointed as an 
attorney. 
Provided that an attorney of one party to a case shall not also 
become attorney of the opposite party to the same case.

> **Provided that an attorney of one party to a case shall not also 
become attorney of the opposite party to the same case.**
