# Section 143: Service of notice or order on appellant or respondent as if it were 
a summons

**Act:** The National Criminal Procedure (Code) Act, 2017  
**Chapter 14:** Provisions Relating to Appeal and Reference for Sanction  
*Page 151*

---

The notice or order to be issued by the court to the 
appellant or respondent pursuant to Section 140, 141 or 142 shall be 
served in accordance with Chapter-6 as if it were a summons.
