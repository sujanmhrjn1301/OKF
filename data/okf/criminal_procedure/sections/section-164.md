# Section 164: Imprisonment for government claimed amount

**Act:** The National Criminal Procedure (Code) Act, 2017  
**Chapter 15:** Provisions Relating to Execution of Judgments  
*Page 170*

---

### Sub-section (1)
The 
concerned person shall pay any government claimed amount or any 
other amount held by a court judgment to be recoverable.

### Sub-section (2)
If such person fails to pay the government claimed 
amount or other amount referred to in sub-section (1), such amount 
shall be recovered by forfeiting his or her property.

### Sub-section (3)
If any government claimed amount or other amount 
could not be recovered even by making forfeiture referred to in sub-
section (2), the sentence of imprisonment for a term not exceeding 
the following term for the following claimed amount or such amount 
shall be set for such person:

- **Clause (a)**: One month for the claimed amount of five 
thousand to ten thousand rupees,
- **Clause (b)**: Three months for the claimed amount of ten 
thousand to twenty-five thousand rupees,
- **Clause (c)**: Six months for the claimed amount of twenty-five 
thousand to fifty thousand rupees,
- **Clause (d)**: One year for the claimed amount of fifty 
thousand to one hundred thousand rupees,
- **Clause (e)**: Six months for every fifty thousand rupees over 
one hundred thousand rupees.

### Sub-section (4)
One shall be sentenced to imprisonment for an amount 
less than that set forth in clause (a) of sub-section (3) in a manner to 
set off three hundred rupees for each day.

### Sub-section (5)
Notwithstanding anything contained elsewhere in this 
Section, in sentencing to imprisonment for a government claimed 
amount, such sentence of imprisonment shall not be set to exceed 
seven years.
