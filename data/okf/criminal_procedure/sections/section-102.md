# Section 102: Examination of expert witness

**Act:** The National Criminal Procedure (Code) Act, 2017  
**Chapter 11:** Provisions Relating to Examination of Evidence  
*Page 115*

---

If an expert who expresses an 
expert opinion in any case related to a heinous or grievous offence in 
which the Government of Nepal is plaintiff dies or has resided 
abroad for a long time and is not able to return to Nepal or it is not 
possible to examine him or her for any other reason, expert opinion 
may be obtained from another expert in the same field who is equal 
to or superior to such expert witness.
