# Section 153: Postponement of execution of judgments

**Act:** The National Criminal Procedure (Code) Act, 2017  
**Chapter 15:** Provisions Relating to Execution of Judgments  
*Page 156*

---

### Sub-section (1)
The court shall 
postpone the execution of a judgment in the following circumstance:

- **Clause (a)**: If an appeal is made in a case which is appealable 
pursuant to law, until the case is finally adjudicated 
on appeal, or if no such appeal is made, until the 
time-limit allowed for preferring appeal is expired,
- **Clause (b)**: If the case is one requiring reference for sanction 
pursuant to law, until the reference made for 
sanction is decided.

### Sub-section (2)
When an appeal is made pursuant to clause (a) of sub-
section (1), the appellant shall submit a petition stating that matter to 
the concerned court.

### Sub-section (3)
Notwithstanding anything contained in sub-section (1), 
if a judgment is made holding any offender liable to a sentence of 
imprisonment, such offender shall be immediately sent to 
imprisonment, except as otherwise provided in this Act.
