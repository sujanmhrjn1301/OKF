# Section 60: Summons may be issued in the name of witness

**Act:** The National Criminal Procedure (Code) Act, 2017  
**Chapter 6:** Warrant for Arrest and Summons  
*Page 77*

---

In issuing a 
summons in the name of a witness pursuant to this Act, the court 
shall issue the summons, stating the necessary matters, in the form 
referred to in Schedule-28.
