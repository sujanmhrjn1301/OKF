# Section 194: Coordination committee

**Act:** The National Criminal Procedure (Code) Act, 2017  
**Chapter 16:** Miscellaneous  
*Page 194*

---

### Sub-section (1)
There shall be a coordination 
committee as follows, in order to bring effectiveness in the 
investigation and prosecution of cases of offences under Schedule-1 
or Schedule-2, by making coordination, as required, between the 
bodies involved in such investigation and prosecution:

- **Clause (a)**: Attorney General 
-Chairperson
- **Clause (b)**: Secretary, Ministry of Law, Justice and 
Parliamentary Affairs 
 
-Member
- **Clause (c)**: Secretary, Ministry of Home Affairs 
-Member
- **Clause (d)**: Inspector General of Police, Nepal Police 
-Member
- **Clause (e)**: Such government attorney of gazetted first 
class level or above in the Office of 
Attorney General as designated by the 
Attorney General 
-Member 
secretary

### Sub-section (2)
The rules of procedures of the coordination committee 
shall be as determined by itself.
