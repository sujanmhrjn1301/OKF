# Section 50: Endorsement to be made in cases of lacking jurisdiction or 
failing to meet requirements

**Act:** The National Criminal Procedure (Code) Act, 2017  
**Chapter 5:** Provisions Relating to Preliminary Proceedings of Cases  
*Page 60*

---

### Sub-section (1)
If, it appears to the court, from 
the contents of any charge sheet or complaint made to it, that the 
court is not competent to proceed and adjudicate the case complained 
or that the complaint does not meet the requirements, the court shall 
make an endorsement to that effect in that charge sheet or complaint.

### Sub-section (2)
In the case of a complaint which is endorsed pursuant to 
sub-section (1) by the reason of the lack of jurisdiction, where the 
day on which such endorsement is made is the deadline of the statute 
of limitation, the proper court shall, notwithstanding anything 
contained in the law in force, register the complaint in accordance 
with law if it is made not later than fifteen days after that day 
excluding the time required for journey.
