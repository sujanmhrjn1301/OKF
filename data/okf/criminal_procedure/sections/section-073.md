# Section 73: Petition may be made to appeal hearing court

**Act:** The National Criminal Procedure (Code) Act, 2017  
**Chapter 7:** Provisions Relating to Detention, Bail and Guarantee  
*Page 92*

---

A person who is 
not satisfied with the order of detention, bail/bond, guarantee or bank 
guarantee made pursuant to this Chapter may make a petition to the 
court hearing appeal up to one level. 
 
 
Provided that in relation to a question as to the illegality of the 
order of detention made in a case involving an offence punishable by 
a sentence of imprisonment for life or imprisonment for a term of 
five years or more, a petition may also be made to the court hearing 
appeal more than one level.
