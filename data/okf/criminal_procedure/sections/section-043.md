# Section 43: Filing of cases

**Act:** The National Criminal Procedure (Code) Act, 2017  
**Chapter 4:** Jurisdiction of Court  
*Page 54*

---

### Sub-section (1)
The following authority or person may file the 
following case required to be filed under this Act in the concerned 
court:

- **Clause (a)**: The concerned government attorney, in relation to a 
case related to any offence under Schedule-1 or 
Schedule-2, in the form of a charge sheet,
- **Clause (b)**: The Government of Nepal, constitutional body, court or 
any public authority, in relation to a case related to any 

 
 
offence under Schedule-3, in the form of a compliant or 
report,
- **Clause (c)**: The concerned person or one who has locus standi to 
file case, in relation to a case related to any offence 
under Schedule-4, in the form of a plaint.

### Sub-section (2)
If a case is filed pursuant to sub-section (1), such case 
shall be deemed to have been filed under this Act.
