# Section 88: Remaining on recognizance after examination of evidence not 
necessary

**Act:** The National Criminal Procedure (Code) Act, 2017  
**Chapter 9:** Provisions Relating to Attorney  
*Page 100*

---

### Sub-section (1)
Notwithstanding anything contained elsewhere in this 
Section, if any party does not wish to remain on recognizance after 
the examination of evidence, the party may make a petition to the 
court for a leave to that effect.

### Sub-section (2)
If a petition is made pursuant to sub-section (1) and the 
court does not consider necessary to keep him or her on 
recognizance, it may discontinue his or her recognizance.
