# Section 75: Forfeiture of bail/bond

**Act:** The National Criminal Procedure (Code) Act, 2017  
**Chapter 7:** Provisions Relating to Detention, Bail and Guarantee  
*Page 93*

---

### Sub-section (1)
If a person who has furnished a 
bail/bond pursuant to this Chapter fails to appear in the court, the 
bail/bond furnished by him or her shall be forfeited.

### Sub-section (2)
If any immovable property has been furnished as a 
bail/bond pursuant to sub-section (1), the property equal to the 

 
 
amount to be paid by such person shall be sold or recovered and the 
rest property shall be returned to him or her.
