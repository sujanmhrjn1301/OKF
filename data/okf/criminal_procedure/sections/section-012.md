# Section 12: Power to form special team to conduct investigation

**Act:** The National Criminal Procedure (Code) Act, 2017  
**Chapter 2:** Provisions Relating to Information on Commission of, and Investigation into, Offences  
*Page 18*

---

### Sub-section (1)
Notwithstanding anything contained elsewhere in this Chapter, if the 
Inspector General of Police or the pertinent superior authority of the 
investigating authority is of the opinion that it is appropriate to form 
a separate investigating team to conduct investigation into any 
offence set forth in Schedule-1 or Schedule-2 of this Act by the 
reason that such investigation cannot be conducted by the designated 
investigating authority alone in view of the gravity of that offence, 
the Inspector General of Police or such superior authority may form 
an investigating team consisting of his or her subordinate employees.

### Sub-section (2)
Notwithstanding anything contained in sub-section (1), 
if it is required to form an investigating team consisting of other 
persons also, in addition to his or her subordinate employees, the 
pertinent superior authority of such investigating authority or the 
Inspector General of Police may, in consultation with the Attorney 
General on that matter, form an investigating team consisting of 
experts as well, and cause such investigation to be conducted by that 
team.

### Sub-section (3)
If an investigating team is formed pursuant to sub-
section (1) or (2), such team shall conduct investigation, in exercise 
of the powers conferred to the investigating authority under this 
Chapter.

### Sub-section (4)
If an investigating team is formed pursuant to sub-
section (1) or (2), the concerned investigating authority shall hand 
over to such team the details of investigation conducted by him or 
her.

### Sub-section (5)
The investigating team formed pursuant to sub-section 
(1) or (2) shall conduct investigation and submit its report to the 
concerned government attorney and give information thereof to the 
concerned police office, as well.

### Sub-section (6)
If, upon receipt of a report pursuant to sub-section (5), 
the government attorney considers it necessary to give necessary 
direction to conduct further investigation on any matter related to the 
offence, he or she may give necessary direction, accompanied by the 
reason therefor, to the investigating team.

### Sub-section (7)
It shall be the duty of the concerned investigating team 
to comply with the direction given by the government attorney 
pursuant to sub-section (6).
