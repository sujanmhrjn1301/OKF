# Section 195: Departmental action to be taken

**Act:** The National Criminal Procedure (Code) Act, 2017  
**Chapter 16:** Miscellaneous  
*Page 194*

---

A public servant who fails to do 
act or take any action required to be done or taken pursuant to this 
Act within the period, if any, specified by this Act shall be liable to 
departmental action in accordance with law.
