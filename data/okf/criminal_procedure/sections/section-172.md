# Section 172: Disposal of documents

**Act:** The National Criminal Procedure (Code) Act, 2017  
**Chapter 16:** Miscellaneous  
*Page 180*

---

### Clause (a)
Complaint, charge sheet or plaint,

### Clause (b)
Statement of the accused or the note of defense,

### Clause (c)
Statement equivalent to the note of defense,

### Clause (d)
Originals of evidence,

### Clause (e)
Memorandum of appeal, if any,

### Clause (f)
Petition for review or revision of the case, if any,

### Clause (g)
Judgment or final decision made by the court,

### Clause (h)
Map, inventory form, statements of property,

### Clause (i)
Such other documents as considered necessary to be 
preserved in accordance with law.

**Explanation:**
Explanation: For the purposes of this Section, the term "final 
decision” means the decision made by the appeal hearing court in 
relation to a judgment appealed for, and the decision made by the 
court competent to make sanction in relation to a reference judgment.
