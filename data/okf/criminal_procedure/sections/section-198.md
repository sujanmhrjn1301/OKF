# Section 198: Power to make alteration in Schedules

**Act:** The National Criminal Procedure (Code) Act, 2017  
**Chapter 16:** Miscellaneous  
*Page 196*

---

The Government of Nepal 
may, as required, make any alteration in or addition to or deletion 
from the offences set forth in Schedule-1 or Schedule-2, by a 
notification in the Nepal Gazette.
