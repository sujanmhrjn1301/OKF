# Section 169: Cases to be tried by District Court

**Act:** The National Criminal Procedure (Code) Act, 2017  
**Chapter 15:** Provisions Relating to Execution of Judgments  
*Page 179*

---

The case trying authority, if 
any, designated by the Government of Nepal, by a notification in the 
Nepal Gazette, shall try any of such cases set forth in the Penal Code, 
and the District Court shall try the other cases.
