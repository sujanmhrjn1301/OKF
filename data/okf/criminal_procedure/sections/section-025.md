# Section 25: Police diary to be maintained

**Act:** The National Criminal Procedure (Code) Act, 2017  
**Chapter 2:** Provisions Relating to Information on Commission of, and Investigation into, Offences  
*Page 35*

---

### Sub-section (1)
Every police officer conducting 
investigation under this Chapter shall maintain a police diary, in the 
form referred to in Schedule-18, in which every proceeding made by 
him or her in the course of investigation shall be recorded 
immediately.

### Sub-section (2)
Such police officer shall record the following matters in 
the diary maintained pursuant to sub-section (1):

- **Clause (a)**: The date and time of receipt of information about the 
offence, and the resume thereof,
- **Clause (b)**: The activities carried out by him or her in the course of 
investigation,
- **Clause (c)**: The place or scene which he or she has visited in the 
course of investigation,
- **Clause (d)**: The exhibits, proofs, evidence or other necessary things 
found in the course of investigation,
- **Clause (e)**: Details of the person, if any, arrested in the course of 
investigation,
- **Clause (f)**: The name, address of each person whom he or she has 
interrogated in the course of investigation, and matters 
stated by such person in relation to the offence,
- **Clause (g)**: Nature of the person arrested or inquired,
- **Clause (h)**: Other relevant fact or information obtained in the course 
of investigation of the offence.

### Sub-section (3)
The government attorney or court may, as required, 
procure and examine the police diary maintained pursuant to in sub-
section (1).

### Sub-section (4)
The authority higher than the concerned investigating 
authority may, if he or she so wishes, inspect the matters set forth in 
the diary maintained pursuant to sub-section (1) and also give 
necessary direction in connection with the investigation.
