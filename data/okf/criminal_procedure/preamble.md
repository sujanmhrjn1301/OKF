# Preamble — The National Criminal Procedure (Code) Act, 2017

**Act Number:** 37 of the year 2017  
**Date of Authentication:** 2017-10-16  
**Commencement Date:** 2018-08-17  

## Enactment Statement
An Act Made To Amend And Consolidate Laws Relating to Procedures of Criminal Cases.

## Preamble
Whereas, it is expedient to make the procedural law simplified and 
timely, by amending and consolidating the laws in force relating to 
procedures on investigation, prosecution, filing, proceeding, hearing and 
adjudication of criminal cases and other procedures related thereto, and 
execution of judgments on such cases; 
Now, therefore, the Legislature-Parliament referred to in clause (1) 
of Article 296 of the Constitution of Nepal has enacted this Act.
