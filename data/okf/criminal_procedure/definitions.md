# Statutory Definitions — The National Criminal Procedure (Code) Act, 2017

*Extracted from Section 2 (Definitions) &bull; Page 1–4*

Unless the subject or the context otherwise requires, in this Code:

### (a) **"court"**
the Supreme Court, High Court or District 
Court, and this term also includes any other court, judicial 
body or authority empowered by law to proceed, hear and 
adjudicate any specific type of criminal cases

### (b) **"investigating authority"**
an authority designated under 
this Act or competent authority under law to make 
investigation into any offence, and this term also includes an 
investigation team formed to investigate into any specific 
offence

### (c) **"Penal Code"**
the National Penal (Code) Act, 2017

### (d) **"complaint"**
a plaint to be filed in a court, and this term 
also includes a charge-sheet in relation to any offence under 
Schedule-1 or Schedule-2, and a report thereon in relation to 
any other case

### (e) **"offence"**
an act punishable by the Penal Code or other 
Act

### (f) **"law"**
a law for the time being in force

### (g) **"grave offence"**
any offence punishable by a sentence 
of imprisonment for a term of more than three and less than 
ten years

### (h) **"heinous offence"**
any offence punishable by a sentence 
of imprisonment for life or for a term of more than ten years,

### (i) **"place"**
a house, shed, cow shed, elephant shed or tent, 
and this term also includes a motor vehicle, vessel, aircraft, 
and other place or means of similar nature

### (j) **"judge"**
the authority of a court to proceed, hear and 
adjudicate a case, and this term also includes an authority 
competent to proceed, hear and adjudicate a criminal case

### (k) **"police office"**
a police office authorized to investigate 
into any offence set forth in Schedule-1

### (l) **"criminal case"**
a case related to an offence under 
Schedule-1, Schedule-2, Schedule-3 or Schedule-4, and this 
term also includes any other case, except a case on any of the 
following matters: 
(1) 
A case related to a legal right, status, post, family 
relationship or property, 
(2) 
Establishment of relationship, or divorce, 
(3) 
Fee, remuneration, salary, allowance or wage, 
(4) 
 Partition, 
succession, 
donation, 
gift, 
will, 
guardianship, curatorship, parental authority, 
adoption, 
(5) 
Any right or claim under any contract, quasi-
contract or unlawful enrichment, 
(6) 
Any right or claim relating to tort, quasi-tort or 
defective product, 
(7) 
Servitude, 
(8) 
Compensation, or 
(9) 
Any other right or liability of civil nature

- (1) A case related to a legal right, status, post, family

- (7) Servitude
- (8) Compensation, or
- (9) Any other right or liability of civil nature

### (m) **"case"**
a criminal case

### (n) **"adjudicating authority"**
an authority empowered by 
law to proceed, hear and adjudicate a case, and this term also 
includes, in the case of a person taken in custody, a court 
nearby the place where the person is taken in custody

### (o) **"Local Level"**
a Village Body (Gownpalika) or 
Municipality

### (p) **"government attorney"**
the Attorney General, Deputy 
Attorney General, Joint Attorney, Deputy Attorney, District 
Attorney or Assistant District Attorney, and this term also 
includes an officer designated by the Attorney General to act 
in the capacity of government attorney
