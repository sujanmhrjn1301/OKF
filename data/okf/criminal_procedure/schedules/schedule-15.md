Note bene: 
(1) 
Post mortem shall be conducted by a forensic expert, to the 
extent possible, and by a trained doctor if such expert is not 
available. 
(2) 
The report shall be prepared by the autopsy conducting expert 
or doctor himself or herself. 
(3) 
The report shall be computer typed to the extent possible, if not 
so, be prepared in a legible manner. In addition, the original of 
the autopsy report shall be attached. 
(4) 
If the specified space is not enough to contain the details, 
details shall also be set down in a separate sheet as well.
