# Schedule 25: (Relating to sub-section (7) of Section 58)

**Act:** The National Criminal Procedure (Code) Act, 2017  

*Page 236*

---

35-day warrant for arrest issued by the court  
 
Whereas, in the case of...............(state the offence) in which the Government 
of Nepal is plaintiff on the information report of ...................... and you/you 
et al................are defendant, a warrant for arrest has been issued for 
arresting you but you have not been arrested; 
Now, therefore, this 35-day warrant for arrest has also been posted. If you 
voluntarily appear or are arrested and produced before this court within 
thirty-five days excluding the time required for journey, you shall be heard 
and tried in accordance with law. If you remain absconding thereby expiring 
the time-limit, the case shall be tried in accordance with law; and you shall 
not be entitled to make a complaint subsequently.
